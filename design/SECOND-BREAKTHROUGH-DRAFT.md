## What this stretch needs to accomplish

The accepted direction keeps the village as the base. Foundation opens deeper local leads and contact with cultivators. The player can learn useful introductory practices for body cultivation, sword cultivation, earth attunement, and formations, then choose the first class together with the second breakthrough. This establishes the first part of a cumulative class history. Supporting techniques remain learnable where compatible; concrete compatibility rules remain open. Combat and sect membership remain optional.

The accepted shared local investigation makes those choices tangible. Preserve the requirement to use the intended direction's introductory practice before choosing it; the four introductions must be explained and available, with sampling the others optional. They primarily come from recovered texts in the accessible outskirts, established through fieldwork. The second breakthrough is a prepared milestone, followed by using its new capability to cross. ## Accepted shared investigation: the ridge waystation

An old ridge waystation is cut off by disturbed qi. The accessible outskirts must now introduce the four starting directions, and the chosen first class must provide a viable way through after the second breakthrough. The village remains the base. The proposed explanation is a long-unused route disrupted by pulses from damaged protective workings; that cause and the individual encounters are still in design. Foundation would let the protagonist perceive enough to begin practical work and observation on the accessible outskirts.

The accepted payoff direction is a first substantial lead toward another connected region, motivating wider travel while unfinished local opportunities remain available. Q85 accepts surviving route records as the form of that discovery; exact contents remain open. Q92 accepts preparing and following that route before breakthrough 3, with an accessible destination and ordinary return travel for every starting class. The third breakthrough remains fully achievable locally. Q93 accepts a small market settlement on the outer Cloudwell basin as the first destination, offering visitor work/trade, an observation lead, and an independent cultivation contact. Q94 selects a short route-finding investigation on the first journey; once the connection is established, later trips become ordinary known travel. Q95 accepts the old-landslide investigation, using surviving landmarks to identify and trace a walkable bypass. Following the lead does not automatically relocate the residence. The village remains home for this stretch, and the channel and hollow retain their existing uses.

The outer area offers modest problems that can be approached safely enough to learn. An obstacle still needs an understandable prerequisite and useful information; the actual post-foundation obstacle and access rules will be specified with the encounters.

## Accepted four starting-class packages

**Q80 accepts the four broad starting-class packages below**, reconciling the first class with the accepted Body, Sword, Earth, and Formation roster. The table illustrates useful field applications; exact texts, scenes, thresholds, equipment, effects, costs, and durations remain open. The previous three-method draft is preserved below as history.

Recovered teaching in the accessible outskirts supplies each introduction. The player must use the intended direction's introduction before acquiring its class; trying the other three remains optional. An introduction yields a modest useful result, and further fieldwork opens practice toward a stronger capability. All preparation is achievable before the second breakthrough and before reaching the waystation interior.

| Direction | Useful introduction and preparation | First-class benefit after breakthrough 2 | Illustrative crossing and continuing use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Body** | Briefly steady circulation while recovering an outer survey marker; investigate how exertion interrupts it and practise controlled movement with manageable loads. | Reinforce ordinary exertion, giving physical work and exposed exploration a dependable advantage. | Carry the necessary equipment through a short disturbed stretch. Later demanding work benefits too; Iron Marrow offers greater physical development, while Mountain Vessel adds ground-dependent adaptation. |
| **Sword** | Apply qi through contact with an ordinary blade to release a small fastening without damaging its inscription; learn to identify a specific binding and practise controlled contact applications. | Deliver more precise, effective force through blade contact against an understood obstruction. | Make a controlled contact cut that opens a local passage; use the same capability for delicate extraction or mechanisms elsewhere. Distant force remains Spirit Blade's later development. |
| **Earth** | Compare accessible stones and ground currents to locate a stable observation point; test which readings distinguish reliable ground from misleading surface calm. | Read nearby ground currents and mineral structures in greater detail, supporting useful surveying. | Identify a viable line through the disturbed terrain. Later surveys can reveal deposits and useful sites; this does not yet grant terrain manipulation or bodily adaptation to ground currents. |
| **Formation** | Place ordinary markers to steady a small observation area; investigate the effect of changing their placement and test the components of a simple fixed layout. | Establish a limited, temporary fixed arrangement that moderates an understood local disturbance. | Prepare a short crossing with the arrangement; reuse the capability for observation or compatible home work within normal capacity. Broader terrain-anchored workings, durable boundaries, and triggered responses develop in later classes. |

The class grants a useful initial capability and further knowledge-limited development. Its mastery contribution remains a separate qualification earned once on acquisition; repeated practice does not add mastery points. These are lasting class benefits whose first substantial application is the crossing, not single-use keys to that destination.

Every route must be independently sufficient using its own introduction and ordinary investigation. A Sword binding, Earth survey, or Formation layout cannot become a required part of everyone else's crossing. Essential shared information needs an ordinary investigative source. All four introductions remain accessible without a sect, combat, a particular residence, another starting class, or a future class ability. The detailed site must be authored to support these alternatives; this proposal does not establish their numerical balance or playthrough feasibility.

The player prepares an intended class and acquires it with the second breakthrough; earlier introductory discoveries retain value. Revising an acquired class remains an open question. The historical replacement-through-retraining language below does not govern this cumulative model.

## Accepted complete readiness for breakthrough 2

Q82 accepts the chosen teaching sequence and the practical means to perform its breakthrough as sufficient preparation once shared foundation has been earned. No additional common milestone quota or generic cultivation exercise is required.

| Condition | Evidence that satisfies it |
|---|---|
| Use the chosen introduction | Learn the recovered teaching and achieve its useful introductory result. Trying another direction does not substitute for this experience. |
| Establish its field discovery | Obtain the relevant finding about load transfer, a binding, underlying ground currents, or displaced flow. This is a discovered fact from fieldwork, not a separate comprehension test or understanding meter. |
| Demonstrate the developed practice | Complete the manageable exercise unlocked by that finding, using capabilities available before acquiring the class. Exact thresholds remain to be designed. |
| Have the necessary means | Have any equipment, setup, or consumable materials actually needed to perform the breakthrough. A method needing no special item gains no arbitrary purchase requirement. |

Qualifying earlier work counts. Completed discoveries and demonstrations need not be repeated for the deliberate breakthrough attempt; only supplies it actually consumes need to be available. This concerns continuing character state, not retention of post-checkpoint gains after lifespan exhaustion.

No extra broad milestone quota, generic practice gate, currency-balance threshold, home upgrade, sect membership, combat, or other introductory sequence is required. Shared foundation supplies the common basis; the chosen fieldwork and practice establish specific readiness. Breakthrough 3's three-of-five milestone rule remains local to that later advancement.

The player deliberately begins the prepared milestone when ready. Existing activity-time, lifespan, and safety rules apply; successful completion awards the first class, its mastery contributions, and its useful initial capability. Selecting an intended class during preparation does not award those benefits early.

The crossing follows advancement and has its own practical requirements. Required field equipment can be explained beforehand, but entering the waystation or demonstrating the stronger crossing capability cannot be an advancement prerequisite. Exact breakthrough and crossing costs, durations, equipment, scene interactions, and checkpoint capture timing remain open.

## Accepted return access

Q84 accepts that first successful arrival by any of the four methods includes opening a sheltered service passage from inside the waystation. That establishes ordinary known travel between the village and the waystation. Trips still consume normal activity time and lifespan, but do not repeat the original class challenge or require replacing its initial crossing setup on every visit. Q84 settles this return-access rule separately from Q83's initial crossing methods.

Opening the passage belongs to the arrival outcome. It needs no separate repair quest, toll, another class ability, or newly purchased item. The final layout must explain why the interior had to be reached before the passage could be opened; its precise mechanism and geometry remain to be authored. The village remains the current residence.

The service passage bypasses the entry problem rather than repairing the whole disturbed area. Later investigations and side routes can still require their own appropriate preparation. In particular, this passage is separate from breakthrough 3's damaged survey spur and does not satisfy that milestone. All four starting classes receive the same ordinary return access; their distinctive capabilities remain useful on other activities and expeditions.

Access is durable world progress within the current character history and follows the normal checkpoint rules. If lifespan exhaustion restores a checkpoint captured before the first crossing, the later-opened passage is not retained. Ordinary setbacks do not erase it. Travel duration and the remaining site's exact behavior remain open.

## Historical proposal: commitment and the second breakthrough

Commitment precedes the second breakthrough. Its exact event and the preparation that follows still need design. The accepted second breakthrough is a prepared milestone that grants the chosen path's first substantial capability, immediately applied to reaching the waystation. The specific capabilities below remain proposals, not approved requirement lists:

| Chosen path | Proposed signature capability | Application to the shared passage | Continuing opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body Tempering | Sustain reinforcing circulation while moving and carrying a load under disruptive pressure. | Cross the short disturbed stretch with the needed field equipment, relying on the cultivated body. | Reach other exposed or physically demanding places without first altering their surroundings. |
| Intent | Hold a precise effect on a binding whose role has been understood. | Release the diagnosed constriction enough to open a usable gap while preserving the workings worth studying. | Address specific seals, obstructions, or delicate separations once their cause is understood. |
| Formation | Link prepared anchors to redirect a local flow across an area. | Establish a corridor through the disturbance and traverse it. | Prepare other sites for difficult observation or passage where suitable local conditions exist. |

The shared outcome is reaching the waystation and gaining a lead beyond the immediate village surroundings. The distinctive reward is a capability the player can use elsewhere. None of these solutions promises to repair the entire site, permanently remove the disturbance, or produce an additional autonomous worker. Their exact costs, duration, persistence, and return access remain open and must make all three approaches worthwhile.

**Accepted order:** recover teachings and establish their use through fieldwork, use the intended path's introduction, commit before the second breakthrough, satisfy its preparation, achieve the breakthrough, and use the new capability to cross. Players may investigate further or try other introductions before commitment.

The preparation must be achievable before the crossing. Required knowledge, materials, and access cannot depend on the capability awarded by the second breakthrough or on reaching the waystation interior. The crossing demonstrates the transformation; it is not the defining trial that earns it. Exact preparation and crossing requirements remain open.

## Historical proposal: development from introduction to breakthrough

For this worked example, each recovered teaching contains a coherent route toward its first specialized method. Its instructions become meaningful through fieldwork. This is a proposed condition of the texts, not a decision that all recovered teachings are complete or that every later technique is already contained in them.

The proposed commitment is a deliberate decision to adopt a method after using its introduction, opening deeper development under the existing retraining rule. No separate establishment rite is proposed at this point. Its exact interaction remains open.

| Path | What the introduction reveals | Further investigation or experiment | Proposed practice and visible readiness | What the breakthrough adds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body Tempering | Brief steadiness is possible, but circulation falters when exertion changes. | At an accessible observation point, compare the pulse while still and while moving; discover where changes of posture interrupt the exercise. | Practise keeping circulation continuous through a controlled movement and recovery sequence. Readiness means sustaining that sequence under the known, mild outer conditions. | Sustained reinforcement under the stronger pressure of the passage, while moving with the necessary equipment. |
| Intent | A precise release can preserve the object around it, but not every visible qi strand causes the obstruction. | Follow the outer workings through their changing pulses and distinguish the constricting binding from the traces it merely produces. | Practise directing the technique at the identified binding at the right phase. Readiness means repeatable, precise contact as the outer pattern changes; holding the larger effect still lies beyond the current method. | Hold the diagnosed constriction released long enough to make a usable passage. |
| Formation | One small area can be steadied, but displaced pressure appears elsewhere. | Observe several accessible points and move a small test arrangement to learn where its displaced flow goes. | Prepare a supported layout and practise tuning its anchors individually. Readiness means tested components and a layout justified by the observed flow; sustaining the linked arrangement comes with advancement. | Maintain the linked anchors together and redirect the flow through a corridor. |

These are concrete readiness proposals, not extra defining trials. They describe preparation that existing activities could establish before a deliberate breakthrough. Their exact checks, durations, materials, and failure behavior are still in design. Each route must work with its own introduction and ordinary investigation alone.

The distinctions to test are physical continuity under changing exertion, identifying and acting on a precise cause, and arranging conditions across a place. Giving every route the same study bar with different labels would not demonstrate those differences. The proposed fieldwork should change what the player understands and can practise. Q61 accepts knowledge limits throughout the campaign; [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-knowledge-and-practice.md) records that rule. It does not yet approve these particular observations, preparation sequences, or effects.

## Historical proposal: scope and consistency checks

The proposed content centers on one local destination with approachable outer problems, three introductory applications, and three approaches to a shared culmination. These are authored variations within the same investigation. Exact scene, activity, item, and character counts remain open.
