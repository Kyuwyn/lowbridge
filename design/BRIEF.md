The cultivator performs one primary activity at a time: work, practise, prepare, or explore. Home improvements benefit the activity underway. At home, an activity stops when its chosen objective is reached, then pauses for the player's next decision. The first major breakthrough is targeted after a few short visits, roughly within the first day at ordinary play speed. This remains an untested pacing target, with no calendar deadline; exact durations and offline processing details remain open.

If a configured activity reaches its objective or an expedition pauses at a meaningful discovery while the player is away, remaining unused away time is banked. The player can use it to accelerate activities chosen after returning. Accelerated activities consume lifespan normally and retain the agreed stops for objectives, meaningful decisions, and automated lifespan protection. The bank has a generous fixed cap intended to cover a few days without upgrades, with its exact value to be tested. Acceleration is enabled for one current objective and ends at that objective or a meaningful pause; the next activity requires enabling it again. Whenever no activity runs, unused real time accrues up to the cap whether the game is open or closed, including completed objectives, decision pauses, and voluntarily stopped activities. Time already used to advance activity is not also credited. The current bank balance remains across checkpoint retries. Exact speed and cap values, presentation, and implementation details remain open.

Throughout the campaign, practice develops what the cultivator currently understands; discoveries and new teachings open deeper growth at meaningful limits. Q61 extends the opening knowledge-limit rule to later cultivation. In the first arc, ordinary expeditions are limited by preparation and access through equipment, techniques, and understood obstacles, alongside their cost in time and lifespan. The opening has no additional energy or food meter. An ordinary investigation obstacle pauses with a useful finding and preserves discoveries, leaving the player to choose how to prepare; its cost is time already spent.

Immediately after the first breakthrough, the surrounding world deepens: new abilities open local leads and first contacts with cultivators, while the village remains the base. Later relocation remains possible. Players learn introductory practices with real uses in investigations or practical problems before committing. One path is committed at a time; changing it requires a substantial undertaking of deliberate retraining and preparation, with clear costs. Retraining preserves discoveries, home development, general advancement, and earned lifespan extensions while the new path's advanced abilities are developed. Exact costs and the treatment of individual former techniques remain open.

Sect membership is optional. Joining offers distinctive teaching and opportunities, while a cultivator with any supported class history can reach ascension independently. A small recurring cast develops through shared experiences and consequential choices, opening further stories or leads. Specific sects, membership terms, characters, and relationship arcs remain in design.
