# First playable implementation: 18 October 2026, revised 20 October 2026

Scope: cold start in Lowbridge through Foundation and its decision pause. Plain DOM, TypeScript, JSON content, Bun build/serve/test. No runtime framework, classes, waystation, or later line modules. Combat is ADR 0026's rolled swings on Speed clocks. The frozen review package remains untouched.

The owner confirmed all three v2 defaults: qi is a reservoir; route rewards retain their separate sources through B2; the starred class additions are accepted for later content.

The 20 October revision lands design plan ADRs 0021-0025: six derived fields rebuilt from authoritative history, with `X.mod.Y` patching what a record pays ([0021](decisions/0021-compounding-vocabulary.md)); tempo as `length`, instant chores kept alongside one sustained `fill` earner per good, a bounded waiting list and sustainability guards ([0022](decisions/0022-chore-tempo.md)); learn/attune/use with a scarce attunement place and a locked, warned pair of methods ([0023](decisions/0023-techniques-and-attunement.md)); `t_` route keys carried by methods as the only cross-module history gate ([0024](decisions/0024-route-keys.md)); and the removal of age, lifespan, checkpoints and retry, with save format v6 ([0025](decisions/0025-lifespan-and-checkpoints-cut.md)).

## Engine contract

Use `GameEngine(records, state?)` and export shared types. The core public surface is `state`, `record(id)`, `stat(id)`, `visible(id)`, `availability(id)`, `start(id, objective?)`, `queue(id, objective?)`, `stop()`, `attune(id)`, `unattune(id)`, `advance(seconds)` and `accelerate()`. `start` completes an instant record on the spot and puts a sustained one in the slot; the tempo is read from `length`, not from the caller. `availability` returns `{ok, reason}`, and a technique without a place reports that as its reason. Objectives are absolute repetition, family-level or resource targets, with a sensible single-completion/next-level default. The UI and route runner call this same surface; they never implement rewards.

State is authoritative history only: stats (value, exp, reps and the run flags), one active task with progress and objective, the waiting list behind it, activity seconds, bank seconds and cap, the ordered attuned list, log, visible-history and locked ids. The six derived fields are never stored. New visibility is remembered, so a visible gate does not disappear when a resource is spent. There is no age, lifespan or checkpoint.

Elapsed time advances the saved activity only to its objective or next meaningful discovery; the unused remainder becomes bank, never both. Bank seconds accelerate only the chosen current objective. An exhausted bank leaves unfinished work at ordinary speed. Each next objective needs acceleration re-enabled. A failed attempt costs the time and preparation it spent and keeps every discovery, level and attuned method; nothing restores an earlier state. Foundation pauses and opens the road on.
