Scope: cold start in Lowbridge through Foundation, then the ridge chapter to the waystation: four teachings with a method each, four exclusive First Directions and their classes, the crossings and the waystation's leads. Plain DOM, TypeScript, JSON content, Bun build/serve/test. No runtime framework, sects or later line modules. Combat is ADR 0026's rolled swings on Speed clocks. The frozen review package remains untouched.

The owner confirmed all three v2 defaults: qi is a reservoir; route rewards retain their separate sources through B2; the starred class additions are accepted for later content.

The 20 October revision lands design plan ADRs 0021-0025: six derived fields rebuilt from authoritative history, with `X.mod.Y` patching what a record pays ([0021](../docs/architecture/adr-0021-compounding-vocabulary.md)); tempo as `length`, instant chores, a bounded waiting list and sustainability guards ([0022](../docs/architecture/adr-0022-chore-tempo.md)); learn/attune/use with a scarce attunement place and a locked, warned pair of methods ([0023](../docs/architecture/adr-0023-techniques-and-attunement.md)); `t_` route keys carried by methods as the only cross-module history gate ([0024](../docs/architecture/adr-0024-route-keys.md)); and the removal of age, lifespan, checkpoints and retry, with save format v6 ([0025](../docs/architecture/adr-0025-lifespan-and-checkpoints-cut.md)). The 27 October revision follows the design audits: chapter one's sustained earners give way to passive rates (0022 amended), the ridge gains a method per direction and reads the school keys (0023, 0024 amended), fights show an exact win chance and graded verdict (0026 amended), and locks apply at payment while discoveries never stop activity ([0027](../docs/architecture/adr-0027-locks-at-payment-and-stops.md)).

## Engine contract

Use `GameEngine(records, state?)` and export shared types. The core public surface is `state`, `record(id)`, `stat(id)`, `visible(id)`, `availability(id)`, `start(id, objective?)`, `queue(id, objective?)`, `stop()`, `attune(id)`, `unattune(id)`, `advance(seconds)` and `accelerate()`. `start` completes an instant record on the spot and puts a sustained one in the slot; the tempo is read from `length`, not from the caller. `availability` returns `{ok, reason}`, and a technique without a place reports that as its reason. Objectives are absolute repetition, family-level or resource targets, with a sensible single-completion/next-level default. The UI and route runner call this same surface; they never implement rewards.

State is authoritative history only: stats (value, exp, reps and the run flags), one active task with progress and objective, the waiting list behind it, activity seconds, bank seconds and cap, the ordered attuned list, log, visible-history and locked ids. The six derived fields are never stored. New visibility is remembered, so a visible gate does not disappear when a resource is spent. There is no age, lifespan or checkpoint.

Elapsed time advances the saved activity to its next stop (objective, full store, unaffordable drain), then the waiting list, then Rest; only time with nothing to run becomes bank, never both. Discoveries never stop it ([ADR 0027](../docs/architecture/adr-0027-locks-at-payment-and-stops.md)). Bank seconds accelerate only the chosen current objective. An exhausted bank leaves unfinished work at ordinary speed. Each next objective needs acceleration re-enabled. A failed attempt costs the time and preparation it spent and keeps every discovery, level and attuned method; nothing restores an earlier state. Foundation raises a banner and opens the road on without pausing anything.
