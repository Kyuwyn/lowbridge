An ordinary villager at the edge of the wilds finds an incomplete cultivation method. It reveals qi but cannot establish a stable practice. Investigating familiar places, improving a modest home, and learning from the world let the protagonist reconstruct a foundation. They later build a history of cultivation classes, travel through connected regions, and make a finite life long enough to earn immortality.

The accepted destination is ascension beyond the mortal world, followed by an epilogue. Discovery and consequential specialization are equal pillars. Wonder, earned mastery, danger, and recurring relationships give the climb its character.

The accepted presentation is a **desktop-first browser game carried by text and activity panels**, with atmospheric art supporting descriptions, discoveries, choices, and progression. Phone support is secondary. Visual style and technology remain open.

## What a short visit feels like

The accepted rhythm is **prepare, investigate, discover, change practice, advance**. The player follows a promising lead, chooses the means to pursue it, and leaves a known activity running toward a chosen objective. A useful finding changes what they can learn or attempt next.

An expedition has a destination and purpose. It handles known work between visits and pauses when a discovery creates a meaningful new choice. Home activities stop at their chosen objective. Throughout the campaign, practice develops what is understood; discoveries and new teachings open deeper growth at meaningful limits. [ADR 0013](../docs/architecture/adr-0013-knowledge-and-practice.md) records this campaign-wide rule. In the opening, obstacles preserve discoveries and explain what preparation or understanding is missing.

Simulated activity consumes lifespan. Pauses do not. Breakthroughs and longevity opportunities extend the available life, with enough room intended for curiosity and reasonable mistakes. Automation stops before exhausting life; a final undertaking requires a deliberate choice.

Unused real time while no activity runs accumulates in a bank, whether the game is open or closed. The fixed cap should cover a few days, with the value to be tested. The player can spend stored time to accelerate one current objective; acceleration ends at the objective or a meaningful pause. Accelerated activity costs normal lifespan, and time used for activity is never also credited to the bank.

If life is exhausted, retry restores character and world state to the last major breakthrough, or the opening starting state before foundation. The **current time-bank balance stays current**: spent time remains spent and later accrual remains available. Post-checkpoint discoveries and character gains do not survive that restore. Ordinary investigation obstacles, failed trials, and unsuccessful combat while lifespan remains have their own smaller setbacks; they do not trigger this reset automatically.

## The first playable arc

The accepted opening package is bounded: **one village and home, two nearby investigations, village errands, perception practice, field equipment, an optional home practice upgrade, and method practice leading to shared foundation**. Exact item and action counts within that package remain open. The detailed proposal is in the [opening working design](OPENING-DRAFT.md).

The incomplete method should reveal qi early in the first session. It makes two local phenomena worth investigating:

| Investigation | Accepted emphasis and reward | Proposed route to a complete method |
|---|---|---|
| Damaged stream channel | Practical preparation; a useful home arrangement. | Basic perception identifies the lead. Ordinary work and field equipment improve access. Observations suggest an arrangement and exercise; practice and a return investigation complete it. |
| Sheltered hollow | Better perception; a technique useful in the field. | An accessible first observation expands what perception practice can teach. Better perception supports deeper observation, an exercise, and reconstruction of a reliable method. |

**Either investigation independently suffices for foundation.** Neither may require the other's reward. The second investigation remains useful afterward, and neither opening route commits a cultivation path. A prepared first breakthrough succeeds and opens deeper local leads and contact with cultivators.

The opening has no additional food or energy meter. Equipment, learned techniques, understood obstacles, and activity time supply its preparation decisions. Names, method provenance, individual scenes, effects, readiness values, prices, and durations still need concrete design.

Proposed questions for the first playable version to answer:

- Does noticing qi change how the player sees a familiar place, and does the resulting lead feel worth pursuing?
- Does each route offer understandable preparation choices and a complete route to foundation?
- Do field discoveries change practice, rather than leave the player waiting on the same bar?
- Are purchases and home improvements useful enough to justify the work and lifespan invested?
- Do objective stops, meaningful discovery pauses, and banked time support short visits without losing player choices?
- Do lifespan protection and checkpoint retries behave consistently, including preservation of the current bank?
- Does foundation feel like a transformation and make the player curious about the next local leads?

These are proposed evaluation questions, not evidence that pacing or enjoyment has already been tested.
