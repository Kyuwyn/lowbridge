Updated 17 October 2026. Q67 accepts the five candidates, mastery gates, and contributions in [ADR 0016](../docs/architecture/adr-0016-third-breakthrough-classes.md). The user subsequently accepted the five preparation-and-benefit packages. **Q70 accepts the three-of-five shared milestone package below, counting each once, allowing overlap with class preparation, and crediting qualifying earlier work.** Q86 accepts the teaching-source mix below; Q87 selects recovery/study or a bounded useful task as the acquisition approach. ## Accepted readiness structure and milestones

Q68 accepts common realm readiness combined with preparation for the chosen class. Common readiness draws on a small selection of useful accomplishments from practice, investigations, home development, and crafting or equipment preparation. The player need not complete every accomplishment or one in every category. Class preparation may also satisfy an eligible common accomplishment; overlapping work does not have to be repeated under another label. Q70 selects the five projects below and requires three. This decision applies to breakthrough 3 rather than automatically setting every later breakthrough's structure.

**Accepted threshold: complete three of the following five distinct milestones, alongside all preparation for the chosen class.** Each milestone counts once. The threshold and project directions are accepted working design, not a measured balance result. Descriptive names are not final lore names; detailed exercises, materials, costs, and durations still need development and pacing tests.

| Milestone | Concrete completion condition | Useful result beyond readiness |
|---|---|---|
| **1. A reliable method** | Complete one taught technique's named control demonstration at the level needed for this stage. Suitable demonstrations are listed below; the chosen class's preparation can directly satisfy this milestone. Reading a text alone does not complete it. | A dependable technique or practical skill that remains usable before and after advancement. |
| **2. The returning chime** | Investigate an old signaling chime near the waystation. Observe its delayed qi pulse at two positions relative to a carved pillar, then complete a controlled test that confirms the pulse follows the old relay's direction. The journal records the confirmed explanation. | Understanding of the relay and a lead toward another surviving fixture. Basic qi perception and ordinary handling suffice. |
| **3. A working study space** | Establish a useful study or practice arrangement in the current residence, adapt it to the intended work, and complete its commissioning objective demonstrating that benefit. An already-owned quiet corner or suitable workbench can be adapted instead of replaced. | A functioning home improvement supporting study or practice through normal residence capacity. |
| **4. Reopen the survey spur** | Prepare a rope-and-anchor kit, or adapt equivalent existing equipment, to make a damaged side route near the waystation usable; complete a traversal confirming safe access. Purchasing or crafting the kit are both valid. Merely owning it is insufficient. | A usable route to a small gathering or surveying site and field equipment retained for later work. |
| **5. A useful commission** | Complete a bounded crafting commission: repair and fit a collector's specimen case using a basic recipe and ordinary materials, then pass the job's preservation check and deliver it. The instruction, materials, and job are accessible to every starting class. | Payment and a reusable preparation recipe. This is one authored job within existing work/crafting, not a reputation or commission-board system. |

The chime investigation's confirmation is an explicit observation-and-test objective, not a free-text answer the game must interpret, a real-time timing puzzle, or a random breakthrough roll. Carrying the chime uses basic equipment; it does not also complete the survey-spur milestone. The later fixture is an optional lead, not another prerequisite hidden inside this milestone.

The survey spur is a separate side route, not the main waystation crossing already earned through breakthrough 2. Its gathering site must not be the only source of any required class ingredient. Pill Refiner's materials and the specimen-case recipe remain obtainable without completing this milestone or choosing a particular class. The crafting commission's recipe requires no Alchemy mastery.

### Accepted practice demonstrations

Only one demonstration is needed for common milestone 1. The intended class still requires its own preparation if the player earns this milestone through a different method. **Q91 accepts the following completion benchmarks for the preparation packages.** Exact loads, repetitions, skill thresholds, ingredients, and activity durations remain open.

| Class being prepared | Qualifying demonstration before that class is acquired |
|---|---|
| Iron Marrow | Finish the taught sequence under a manageable load, maintaining circulation through its changes of posture and exertion. The stronger post-breakthrough loads are not required. |
| Mountain Vessel | Match circulation to a sensed ground current and hold a brief, stationary connection under the taught conditions. The prolonged full-cycle reading granted after advancement is not required. |
| Spirit Blade | Complete the taught contact-control exercise, making a controlled cut without disturbing the marked surrounding material. Projected cutting is not required. |
| Earth Array | Configure a small fixed practice layout and manually keep its known pattern steady through the taught adjustments. Its advanced terrain working is not required. |
| Pill Refiner | Complete a beginner extraction run in an ordinary vessel with controlled heating, producing the usable simple extract described by the accessible teaching. Neither Alchemy mastery nor the later conditioning recipe is required. |

The demonstration is the completion condition of the taught practice objective. It does not add a separate examination after the same exercise has already succeeded. The intended class's qualifying demonstration directly satisfies the reliable-method common milestone as well as that part of its practical preparation. A different qualifying demonstration can satisfy the common milestone, but does not waive the intended class's own preparation. Only one common milestone is awarded regardless of how many methods are demonstrated.

Qualifying earlier work counts when it meets the full stated conditions; introductory use alone does not automatically establish those conditions. Accessible missing basics remain included in the accepted teaching. The exercises use pre-advancement capabilities and normal activity controls, without a real-time input test or an additional breakthrough trial. Manual tending describes the cultivator's activity, not timed player inputs. The existing mastery gate, teaching, relevant equipment/material requirements, and three-of-five common readiness threshold remain in force. Crediting an earlier demonstration does not itself grant understanding supplied by a newly recovered teaching. These accepted demonstrations do not settle numerical readiness or measured pacing.

### Counting, overlap, and earlier investments

- Each named common milestone is a once-only condition. Repeating its objective, producing extra copies, or completing a second practice demonstration does not add another common milestone.
- A qualifying objective states which common milestone it completes. It may also satisfy class preparation. For example, completing the controlled contact exercise satisfies the Spirit Blade practice requirement and common milestone 1, with no duplicate exercise.
- A journey or project can contain multiple distinct qualifying outcomes. Investigating the chime and reopening the survey spur are two accomplishments; using a technique and tool while investigating the chime is not three accomplishments. Each objective must have its own outcome and useful result, rather than repeat the same success under another label.
- Credit follows completed conditions, not when the player opens the readiness panel or selects a candidate class. Already-completed qualifying objectives count. Existing equipment, arrangements, and discoveries may supply their requirements without repurchase or rediscovery.
- Opening purchases alone do not complete the new milestones: the quiet corner must meet the study-space commissioning condition, and field equipment must establish the survey-spur access. Adapting an existing asset should require only missing work. If it already meets the full stated condition, award credit without a ceremonial repeat.
- Completion is retained through ordinary activity setbacks, sale or reconfiguration of the supporting equipment/home, and later class browsing. The normal exact-state checkpoint retry still restores the milestone state captured at that checkpoint. This preserves an accomplishment without exempting it from the accepted retry rules.

The list consists of fixed milestone conditions, not spendable points. Finishing the third common milestone does not automatically choose a class or trigger the breakthrough; intended-class readiness and a deliberate attempt are still required.

### Example ways to become ready

| Priorities | Three common milestones | Separate class preparation still required |
|---|---|---|
| Exploration, with no home upgrade or crafting requirement | Reliable method + returning chime + survey spur | Complete the intended class's teaching, practice, and equipment/material requirements. The field kit can be bought. |
| Develop the home and make useful things | Reliable method + working study space + useful commission | Class-specific requirements remain; a crafting job does not grant Alchemy or replace refining instruction. |
| Improve a base and its nearby access | Reliable method + working study space + survey spur | Complete class preparation; no chime investigation or crafting commission is mandatory. |
| Prepare common readiness before mastering the intended method | Returning chime + working study space + useful commission | The chosen class's practical technique must still be developed before attempting the breakthrough. |

All five third-breakthrough candidates can use the exploration route. No milestone requires a sect, combat, a new residence, or a capability first granted by breakthrough 3. None requires completing the other four milestones. This is a prerequisite design check, not proof of balanced time, prices, or lifespan costs. No new food, energy, or relationship-maintenance meter is introduced.

A chosen class then requires its accepted mastery gate, suitable teaching, trained capability within that teaching, and relevant equipment or materials. These can arise through ordinary activities and discoveries. A unique quest for every class is not required. Required sources must be obtainable before choosing, without a mandatory sect, combat encounter, unchosen class, or fixed residence.

Q69 settles how advancement is completed: when the chosen class's preparation and common readiness are satisfied, a deliberate attempt at breakthrough 3 succeeds. There is no additional defining trial here. This leaves space to develop the acquired class's capabilities before the accepted first defining trial at breakthrough 4.

## Accepted teaching sources after reaching the waystation

Q86 places teaching for the five accepted third-breakthrough candidates within the village and waystation surroundings. It mixes recovered records with independent practitioners, giving the recurring cast useful roles. No source requires travel to the next region or relocation; those remain separate opportunities. **The source mix and access boundaries are accepted.** Q87 selects a bounded useful task in exchange for each practitioner's instruction; Q88 accepts the practice-yard observations and guided herb delivery below. Exact documents, characters, locations, recovery/study details, task quantities, resource costs, and durations remain open.

| Candidate | Accepted teaching source | Access needed for the accepted preparation |
|---|---|---|
| **Iron Marrow** | Recovered conditioning notes among the waystation's practical records. | Teach ordinary load-bearing circulation and manageable conditioning; stronger reinforcement is the later class reward. |
| **Mountain Vessel** | Complementary circulation and ground-current records that explain how to connect the two practices. | Supply elementary ground sensing for a Body starter or circulation under exertion for an Earth starter. A missing basic skill must be learnable without acquiring the other starting class. |
| **Spirit Blade** | An independent blade practitioner reachable in the local area. | Teach blade-contact control and the missing basics to a qualifying Formation starter as well as a Sword starter. Sword mastery, projected cutting, sect membership, and combat are not prerequisites for receiving the needed instruction. |

Source access must precede the third-breakthrough readiness quota: an intended class's practical preparation can still satisfy the reliable-method milestone. No source is hidden behind completing the chime, reopening the survey spur, establishing a study space, or delivering the specimen case. A class's mastery gate still applies to acquiring that class; learning basics does not award mastery or substitute for the gate.

Meeting a practitioner or recovering notes supplies a teaching and useful next objectives. It does not automatically finish practical preparation, award a common milestone, or grant the class. Qualifying earlier study and demonstrations receive their existing credit; only missing preparation is required. These source roles introduce no relationship-maintenance or reputation quota. Q90 settles the three recovery/study objectives within those boundaries; Q88 settles the two practitioner task premises below.

The refining introduction remains obtainable after choosing another third-breakthrough class, preserving the accepted later entry into Golden Cauldron. Its later deeper preparation is separate. These source roles do not establish one exclusive NPC per class, a fixed cast size, or the location of all future teaching.

These are character/world-state acquisitions under normal checkpoint restoration, not permanent account unlocks outside the retry rules.

Obtaining instruction still does not complete a practical demonstration, award a common milestone, or grant a class or mastery contribution. Balancing must compare total activity time and lifespan, including travel, recovery/study, the useful task, lessons, and subsequent preparation. The source type alone does not establish an advantage, and no resource costs or pace have been validated.

The opportunities are available around the village and waystation, without requiring the optional survey spur, another class, combat, a new residence, or next-region travel. Their tasks and useful outcomes should be visible before choosing the class. The first application may require its ordinary travel, setup, tools, ingredients, or recipe execution, but no additional mandatory practice tier or teaching acquisition stands between advancement and using the basic benefit. They are optional uses of the reward, not extra conditions for earning the class or the next breakthrough.

These examples do not prescribe five exclusive class quests or five separate routes through a mandatory obstacle. They use existing paid work, investigations, and focused crafting. Benefits persist beyond a single demonstration. Required campaign materials remain obtainable through other accessible sources; none of these optional applications is the sole supplier of a mandatory ingredient. Exact job details, findings, recovered supplies, effects, quantities, and durations remain open. A finding does not start a real-time appointment or demand a timed visit; any waiting within an objective uses ordinary simulated activity time.

Efficiency refers to simulated work for a suitable activity; it does not alter the real-time bank's accounting or give free lifespan. A Pill Refiner preparation supports one selected objective and cannot stack with copies on that same objective. Its total value must account for ingredient acquisition and refining time as well as practice time saved; a net time advantage has not been established. It grants no indefinitely stacking body gains or lifespan extensions. These applications add no energy, food, injury, or recovery meter.

## Contrasting histories to test

**Body → Iron Marrow versus Body → Mountain Vessel:** both reach Body 2. Iron Marrow must offer an appreciable physical advantage now, while Mountain Vessel has Body 2 / Earth 1 and access to terrain-based possibilities. The extra Earth contribution must not come with all of Iron Marrow's physical benefits as well.

**Earth → Mountain Vessel:** finishes with Earth 2 / Body 1. Its preparation builds a missing physical skill through accessible teaching, without needing the Body mastery that the class itself will add. It retains the first Earth class's benefits. This must remain a viable, distinctive route rather than a worse copy of starting with Body.

**Formation → Spirit Blade:** finishes with Formation 1 / Spirit 2 / Sword 1. Existing Spirit expertise meets the gate, while beginner blade instruction supplies the practical preparation. Earlier formation capabilities remain available. This route must not secretly require Sword mastery before acquisition.

**Formation → Earth Array:** finishes with Formation 2 / Spirit 1 / Earth 1. It should feel like deeper formation cultivation through terrain and local qi. The class's benefits must develop formation capability in addition to adding Earth access.

**Sword → Pill Refiner:** finishes with Sword 1 / Spirit 1 / Alchemy 1 / Body 1. It retains sword capability while preparing for alchemical possibilities. Its first refining instruction, vessel, and ingredients must be accessible without Alchemy or Body mastery, combat, or sect membership.

These are design checks on prerequisites and incentives, not playtest results. The existing map's eleven allowed two-class histories remain arithmetically valid; practical accessibility and lifespan costs still need content and testing.
