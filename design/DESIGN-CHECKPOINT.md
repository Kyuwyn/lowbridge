# Design checkpoint: ready to leave the interview

18 October 2026, through Q123. **The shaping phase has enough decisions to move into planning a first playable slice.** The complete eight-breakthrough game is not fully specified, and implementation has not started or been authorized by this checkpoint.

## What is settled

- A finite xianxia idle adventure from an ordinary village life toward immortality, with eight major breakthroughs and an ending.
- One cultivator and one primary activity, knowledge-limited practice, useful exploration, paid work, focused crafting, and a current residence.
- Objective-based activities, meaningful decision pauses, one-for-one banked time with prompt spending, activity-based aging, and breakthrough checkpoints with exact character/world retries.
- Cumulative class history and overlapping masteries: four starter classes, five third-breakthrough candidates, seven fourth-breakthrough candidates, with specialist and hybrid qualifications.
- Progression through the first four breakthroughs: village foundation, ridge waystation, class development and shared projects, then the Cloudwell trial. Teaching sources, practical preparation, noncombat access, and initial class benefits are defined.
- A browser-first, desktop-first presentation centered on text and activity panels. Optional combat and sect membership remain viable choices rather than universal progression requirements.
- A compact working target of roughly 3-5 real days through Cloudwell for the two reference journeys, with foundation within the first day and meaningful decisions normally within 8-12 ordinary activity hours. These are targets to test.

The [campaign outline](CAMPAIGN-OUTLINE.md) consolidates accepted scope. The [worked pacing budget](PACING-PAPER-BUDGET.md) supplies provisional calculations, not final balance.

## First playable slice to plan

Bound the initial build to **the village opening through shared foundation**. Include the fragment and early qi discovery, village work and equipment, home support, both investigation routes, reconstructed cultivation, and the first prepared breakthrough. Support returning visits with save/load, objective stops, banked progress, age, and the opening retry boundary. Keep the interface focused on understandable available work, discoveries, and consequences.

The purpose is to learn whether choosing a lead, preparing for it, making a discovery, and turning that discovery into cultivation is enjoyable and understandable across short visits. It is not necessary to build all later classes or regions to answer that question.

Before coding, turn the existing opening draft into a bounded implementation plan, choose a suitable technical foundation, and identify the minimum content and persistence contracts. Those are ordinary planning tasks; they do not require another long questionnaire.

## What remains

**Authoring and tuning we can draft:** provisional durations and prices, numerical home effects, the age conversion, lifespan extensions, bank capacity, concrete recipes, names, prose, layout, and routine technical choices. Record assumptions and revise them from review or play evidence. The paper model's 76.10/80.45-hour totals, home reduction, visit settings, and fictional-time rate are not individually approved values.

**Substantial later design:** breakthroughs 5-8, their places and challenges, later class development, and the final ascension. The ending and overall campaign duration need development when that portion is reached. Earlier-class revision also remains unresolved; it does not prevent testing the opening.

**Evidence still needed:** a playable opening and actual short-session playtests. Paper calculations cannot establish enjoyment, accessibility, reliable saves, or balanced pacing across every class history.

Return to user questions when a major direction, scope tradeoff, or a finding from play needs a decision, and not for every activity or tuning constant.
