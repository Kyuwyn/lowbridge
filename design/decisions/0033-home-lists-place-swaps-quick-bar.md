ADR 0033: Home lists, one-click place swaps and the quick bar

Status: Accepted. Date: 28 October 2026. Basis: the owner's requests of 28 October (Home sorted by category such as resting and research; pieces that take the same place swap in one click instead of being taken down first; Shift and a number puts an action or technique on a bar at the bottom, as in Arcanum); [ADR 0023](0023-techniques-and-attunement.md) (attunement places, the practice mat's place), [ADR 0027](0027-locks-at-payment-and-stops.md) (§3, the take-down confirmation).

**Context.** Home listed every arrangement in one table in content order, so the two rest pieces, the drill frames and the stores sat interleaved. A place held one piece and refused a second ("The rest place holds Straw pallet; take it down on Home first."), so upgrading the pallet to the cot took a take-down, a confirmation that the pallet's cost was lost, and a second purchase. Rule 6 (*copy*), **exclusive-choice slots: one record per slot, swap to change**, was copied only in its first half. The reference keeps a swapped-out record: the T4 save holds `"atticbedroom": {"disabled": true, "owned": true}` for the home it outgrew (`research/arcanum-reference/03-data-model.md`). Rule 32 (*copy*) names the quick bar among the panels that never leave the screen, and `01-shell-and-layout.md` describes it: ten slots keyed 1 to 0, "Roll-over Item and hold {Shift} + {Number} to assign quickslot. Press {Number} again to use quickslot item." Hundred Days keeps the same binding (`research/hundred-days-reference/04-engine-behind-the-curtain.md`).

**Decision.**

1. **Every arrangement names a `category`**, one of `homeCategories` in `src/content.ts`: Rest, Practice, Study, Herbs, Storage, Craft, shown in that order under Shelter. The validator requires the field on every arrangement and refuses it anywhere else or outside the list. Pieces that share a place share a category, so rivals sit side by side.
2. **Choosing a piece for a filled place swaps it in.** The piece in the place steps aside (`value` 0) and is **kept**: it was bought, so swapping it back costs nothing (`GameEngine.kept`: a place piece that has started and is not installed). Its cost line gives way to "Kept: swaps back in for nothing" and its card reads **Kept · swap in**; the piece that would be swapped out is named in the details ("Swaps out A proper cot, which is kept"). The journal says which piece took the place and which is kept. No confirmation is asked unless the piece stepping aside holds an attunement place a method needs; then `warnText` names the method that would be released, as the take-down confirmation does (ADR 0027 §3).
3. **Place pieces are no longer taken down.** `removable` is floor pieces only, so the Places rows lose their Take down button. Floor pieces keep it, with no refund and the same confirmation.
4. **A place piece pays only while it holds its place.** Because a swap back is free, the validator refuses `length`, `result`, `at` and `every` on a record with a `slot`; its `mod` is what it gives. `onStart` and events fire once and cannot be farmed.
5. **The quick bar.** Ten keys, 1 to 9 then 0, sit at the bottom of every view. Shift and a number binds the action under the pointer, or the one holding keyboard focus, to that key, moving it if another key held it. The number alone, or a click on the key, does exactly what the action's own button does, confirmations included. Number keys are ignored while typing in a field or while a confirmation is open. Each key shows its name and whether it is ready, running, done or blocked, with the reason on hover, and a × clears it. The bar is a preference of this browser (`localStorage`, `lowbridge.ui.hotbar`), like closed groups, not part of the save. While it is empty it shows how to fill it; on a touch screen an empty bar is hidden, since nothing can be pointed at.

**Divergences from *copy* rules, with reasons.**

- **Rule 6** is now copied in full, with one change: Lowbridge has no `disabled` flag. A kept piece is one with `started` set and `value` 0, which saves already hold, so neither the save format nor existing journeys change. A pallet taken down under the old rule is kept from now on.
- **Rule 28 (quickslots as the active layer)** stays as ADR 0031 settled it for fights. The quick bar here is a shortcut to any action or technique, not a way to steer a fight.

**Consequences.** `bun run validate` figures are unchanged: the route runner now treats a kept piece as worth nothing (`productive` in `scripts/routes.ts`), so no route swaps back and forth. The browser smoke checks the order of the Home lists, a paid swap, a free swap back, the floor take-down confirmation, binding a technique with Shift+1 and using it with 1 from another view.

Still open: whether a later home should offer a second place of one kind; a quick bar per journey rather than per browser; binding by touch.
