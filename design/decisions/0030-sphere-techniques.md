ADR 0030: Sphere techniques, opposed pairs and derived titles

Status: Accepted model for the ridge retrofit and the basin road: identity is the set of sphere techniques a cultivator holds; each sphere teaches an opposed pair per tier; a Direction is one sphere-agnostic breakthrough; titles are derived from what is held, never bought. Every number here is content and provisional; the validator bands in [`design/CHAPTER-2-BASIN-ROAD.md`](../CHAPTER-2-BASIN-ROAD.md) are the acceptance. Date: 27 October 2026. Basis: the owner's decisions of 27 October (scope, model A, spine, pacing, delivery), [ADR 0021](0021-compounding-vocabulary.md) (patching, and the flatness it named), [ADR 0023](0023-techniques-and-attunement.md) (learn, attune, use; `held`; the leaf-eight lock), [ADR 0024](0024-route-keys.md) (keys as the seam), [ADR 0026](0026-combat-exchanges.md) §1-2, and rules 6, 11, 14, 15, 54, 71, 73 and 74 of the design rules ([`research/arcanum-reference/12-design-rules.md`](../../research/arcanum-reference/12-design-rules.md), [`research/hundred-days-reference/06-design-rules.md`](../../research/hundred-days-reference/06-design-rules.md)).

**Context.** The ridge ships four exclusive First Direction breakthroughs (`direction_body`, `direction_sword`, `direction_earth`, `direction_formation`), each paying one class record whose `mod` carries the line's whole stat package (Body Disciple: Strike +1, Guard +2, Speed +0.2, Dodge +5, and the Labour patch). Paying one closes the other three. The one-shot lock is the flatness ADR 0021's context named in another form: the choice is made once, at a bill, between four bundles of numbers, and nothing the player holds afterwards can be held differently. The next chapter was to repeat the shape at the Second Direction with eight rival classes (design plan B.2 row 3, B.3), and the owner asked instead that build identity come from **cultivation techniques chosen per sphere**, and that every system grow.

The reference rules at stake:

- Rule 6: **exclusive-choice slots**, *copy*. One record per slot, swap to change.
- Rule 11: **classes are keys that count**, *copy the key/count/exclusive shape; beat the hidden exclusion*.
- Rule 15 and rule 54: hidden consequences early; **irreversible choices must say what they close**, *beat*.
- Rule 71: **identity lives where the numbers live; beat names without payoff**.
- Rule 73: **a closing fork must differ inside the stage that offers it**, *avoid*.
- Rule 74: **never grant a slot or cap that no reachable content can fill**, *avoid*.
- Rule 14 (design plan Part A): a path whose second segment is `mod` patches what an earlier record pays.

**Decision.**

1. **Five spheres, each with a family, a material and a school key.** Body (Circulation, marrow herbs, `t_school_body`), Sword (Blade Intent, whetstone grit, `t_school_sword`), Earth (Ground Reading, veined ore, `t_school_earth`), Formation (Array Craft, inked tokens, `t_school_formation`) and, new on the basin road, Pill (Refining, pale root, `t_school_pill`). A sphere is not a class and closes nothing: a cultivator may carry any number of them, and what they carry is what their play is made of.

2. **Each sphere teaches an opposed pair per tier.** A pair is two `attune: true` techniques that `lock` each other, both carrying `warn` naming the other, both carrying the same sphere key, and at the deep tier a `t_deep_<sphere>` key as well. The ridge tier pairs the four shipped methods with four opposites (*Sink the breath under the load* / *Rise under the load*; *Cut by intent* / *Let the blade wait*; *Read the grain* / *Walk where it slid*; *Fix a layout* / *Open a layout*); the basin tier adds five deep pairs, one per sphere. Attuning one half closes the other for good, the leaf-eight pattern of ADR 0023, so the commitment the class lock used to carry is made inside each sphere, in a choice between two things that differ at once in what they pay (rule 73): one half of every pair is the cautious reading, the other the forward one, and their `held` tables pull different stats. Because the halves lock each other, a pair yields at most one key per tier: `g.t_school_body` counts the opening's two methods plus one ridge half plus one deep half, and `g.t_deep_<sphere>` is 0 or 1.

3. **Class stat payouts move into `held`.** The numbers the class records carried (Strike, Guard, Speed, Hit, Dodge, Strain discount, floor space, Rest and practice rates) now sit in the pair halves' `held` tables and are paid while the half holds an attunement place, as ADR 0023 §2 already pays the opening methods. Holding is the identity (rule 71): releasing the method gives its numbers back, and a place is scarce, so the build is what fits in the places held, not what was once bought. The ridge halves grow from ADR 0023's figures to carry this weight (*Sink the load* Life +2, Guard +2; *Cut by intent* Strike +2, Hit +10; *Read the grain* a richer hand pry and Strain cap +2; *Fix a layout* Rest and breath practice +25%), and the opposites pay the other reading (Speed, Dodge and Strike; Guard, Speed and Dodge; a Strain discount and Focus; a qi rate and a span of floor).

4. **A Direction is one sphere-agnostic breakthrough.** `first_direction` replaces the four exclusive directions and `second_direction` is authored the same way. Its core is a disjunction over sphere families and the sphere's drills (First: any family at 4 with its drills begun; Second: two spheres deepened and three methods held), its pointers read keys and shared stores (Lore, a furnished home, a school method used, wages; later the chime, the spur, a study, a commission, the leaves), and its bill is in the slow shared currencies plus a threshold of the sphere's material held, never spent (rule 12 shape; the material is a `need`, so a player who has traded it must gather it again but is not charged for carrying two spheres). It locks nothing. Its `mod` raises **every** sphere the player carries past the gate, as an expression: `"circulation.max": "3 * (g.circulation >= 4)"` and so on for each family, so the plan's "raises its line's entry cap by +3" (Part A rule 2) now reads "raises every carried line's cap by +3". A Direction grants one attunement place (3 → 4 → 5, amending ADR 0023's schedule) and the bank cap.

5. **Titles are derived, not bought.** The breakthrough carries ordered `events`; every `when` requires the breakthrough owned and a shared `<tier>_title` flag at 0, and each `changes` sets that flag and one title record to 1. The first matching event wins, so the order is the precedence: at the Second Direction the four hybrids precede Pill Refiner, which precedes the four pure titles. A title is `kind: class` with a `title`, revealed by `require: g.<id> > 0`, and `GameEngine.title()` reads it as it reads any class a breakthrough grants. A title record keeps **only** the `X.mod.Y` patches that make a later chapter compound an earlier one (rule 14, ADR 0021's second authoring obligation): Body Disciple keeps `labour.mod.vigor.max`, Earth Listener `stonework.mod.strain.max`, Formation Student the pallet, cot, mat and plot patches, Sword Disciple `cut_timber.yield`; each Second Direction title carries one patch into chapter one or the ridge (Mountain Vessel makes the ground drills teach Circulation, Pill Refiner makes Shu's sorting turn up pale root, and so on). The names still pay (rule 71), but what they pay is the compounding, and the fighting numbers are held.

6. **Keys and the seam.** New keys: `t_school_pill`, five `t_deep_<sphere>`, `t_leaf` (a recovered leaf of the slip), `t_commission` (a commission done). Every one is read by a Direction pointer, a `need` or a sign, as ADR 0024 requires. The basin file gates only on basin ids, shared ids and keys; the records both the ridge and the basin need as vocabulary (`waystation`, the four leads, `first_direction`, `workbench`, `reading_desk`, `cottage`, `coffer`) move unchanged from `ridge.json` to `shared.json`, where ADR 0024's seam check exempts them.

7. **Save v7.** `hobby` and `effects` join the authoritative state ([ADR 0031](0031-basin-road-systems.md)); older saves are refused by name, not migrated, as ADR 0025 established for v6.

**Divergences from *copy* rules, with reasons.**

- **Rule 11 (same-tier choice is exclusive): diverged.** A Direction never locks a rival Direction, and a title is never chosen. Reason: the one-shot class lock was the flatness ADR 0021 named, a choice made once at a bill between four numbers; forks within a sphere and attunement scarcity carry the commitment instead, and they carry it every time a place is filled. The key/count shape of rule 11 is kept whole: `g.t_deep_<sphere>` is an integer read by the next tier's gates, and "three from one line" is still the deepest route.
- **Rule 6 (exclusive slots): copied.** Attunement places are the slot; a pair half swaps in and out of one. The lock between halves is ADR 0023's, with rule 54's per-record `warn` naming what closes.
- **Rule 73: kept.** Every pair differs inside the chapter that offers it; the validator's witness routes hold both halves of every pair and both must be reached.
- **Rule 74: kept.** The fifth place exists only once a route holds five methods; `bun run validate` keeps the places-against-held check from ADR 0023's amendment and now expects five.

**Consequences.** Four breakthrough records and four class `mod` tables leave the ridge; one breakthrough, four opposites, four title records and a `first_title` flag replace them, and the crossings read a held half (`g.sink_the_load.attuned || g.rise_with_load.attuned`) instead of a class. The four families' `perLevel` tables that raised `direction_<sphere>.rate` raise `first_direction.rate` instead, so a player carrying two spheres settles the Direction faster; that is intended. `GameEngine.title()` must count ids named in a breakthrough's event `changes` as granted, not only its `result`. The browser smoke's ridge report, the content tests that name `direction_sword` and the route runner's `RIDGE_ROUTES` all name the removed ids and are rewritten. Chapter one and the ridge's pacing are unchanged: the sphere-agnostic Direction asks the same core, the same three-of-four and the same bill as the four it replaces, and the validator's Foundation and ridge bands stand. The basin road's bands are new and live in the chapter spec.

**Supersessions.**

- **Supersedes [ADR 0015](0015-cumulative-class-masteries.md)**: masteries are now key counts. "Body ≥ 1" reads `g.t_deep_body >= 1` (or `g.t_school_body >= 2` at the ridge tier); there is no separate mastery total, and a class contributes nothing a key does not already say.
- **Supersedes the rosters of [ADR 0016](0016-third-breakthrough-classes.md) and design plan B.3 for the Second Direction**: the eight B3 names (Iron Marrow Adept, Mountain Vessel, Blade Body, Spirit Blade, Earth Array Adept, Deep Listener, Array Keeper, Pill Refiner) become derived titles with their precedence in §5; ADR 0016's five common milestones become the Second Direction's pointers with the leaves as a sixth. [ADR 0017](0017-fourth-breakthrough-choice-breadth.md) (B4) is untouched: it will be rewritten on this model when its chapter is designed.
- **Supersedes design plan Part A rule 2** ("a class … raises its line's entry technique cap by +3") with "a Direction raises every carried sphere's cap by +3 and grants a place", and **rule 6's** "a class choice closes its same-tier rivals" with "a pair's halves close each other; Directions never lock". **Supersedes B.2 rows 2 and 3** (the Direction rows) and **B.3** as the class table.
- **Amends [ADR 0023](0023-techniques-and-attunement.md)**: attunement places run 3 → 4 (First Direction) → 5 (Second Direction); the ridge's "one method per direction" becomes a pair per sphere with revised `held` tables.
- **Amends [ADR 0024](0024-route-keys.md)**: the keys in §6 are added, and the eight shared records named there move to `shared.json`.
- **Amends [ADR 0026](0026-combat-exchanges.md)** §1's "raised by" column: the class figures are now held figures ([ADR 0031](0031-basin-road-systems.md); the stances that ADR first added as a second loadout were withdrawn the same day).
- **Amends [ADR 0025](0025-lifespan-and-checkpoints-cut.md)'s save shape**: v7.

Still open: whether a third tier of pairs should ever occupy two places; whether a released deep half should keep its key (today a key counts owned records, and a locked half was never owned, so release changes nothing); whether the Second Direction's hybrid precedence should be a player choice when more than one hybrid matches; and the B4 roster on this model.
