ADR 0017: Meaningful choice at the fourth breakthrough

Status: Accepted choice-breadth requirement, seven-class working roster, earlier-mastery gates and contributions, Golden Cauldron's later entry into alchemy, all seven broad preparation-and-benefit packages, their teaching source mix, the two practitioner contributions, the shared shelter with three recovered-source study objectives, the seven class practice demonstrations, and their first optional applications. Final names, exact lessons, recipes, numerical effects, costs, and durations remain open. Updated 18 October 2026. Basis: explicit answers Q71-Q73, Q79, and Q108-Q112; shared trial decisions are recorded in ADR 0018.

Every currently valid history through the first two class choices must have at least two meaningful, attainable class options at breakthrough 4. Deliberate preparation may open a distinct alternative. This requirement applies to the eleven histories in the current early map; it does not establish a fixed minimum choice count for the entire campaign.

The original seven-class sketch allowed five histories only one continuation: Body → Iron Marrow; Earth → Mountain Vessel; Earth → Earth Array; Formation → Spirit Blade; and Formation → Earth Array. They were valid advancement routes, but did not provide a fresh class decision at this breakthrough.

Q72 accepts Golden Cauldron's entry from any current class history through accessible foundational refining practice followed by deeper study and practical preparation, without mandatory earlier Alchemy mastery. Qualifying earlier work counts. Newcomers develop extraction and controlled heating before the deeper preparation; existing Pill Refiners retain their benefits and receive credit for work already completed. The class adds only Alchemy +1: prior Pill Refiners reach Alchemy 2 and retain their earlier Body contribution and conditioning benefits, while newcomers reach Alchemy 1 without retroactive Pill Refiner benefits or Body mastery.

Requirements inspect accumulated mastery before this advancement, without additional named-predecessor requirements. Every class also needs accessible teaching and practical preparation. Choosing Golden Cauldron cannot supply Alchemy to qualify for Spirit Alchemist at the same advancement. Practice develops skills rather than awarding class mastery.

These are working names. [ADR 0018](0018-first-defining-trial.md) records Q74-Q78: breakthrough 4 is the first defining trial, using the Cloudwell convergence, three shared approaches, three execution stages with one decision pause, and practical common readiness. The accepted Golden Cauldron entry provides an alchemical alternative for the five formerly single-option histories.

Meaningful alternatives require distinct useful capabilities and feasible teaching, material, time, and lifespan requirements. Counting two eligible names alone is insufficient. All options must respect knowledge-limited practice, independent/noncombat progression, and cumulative class history. Earlier choices and mastery totals still determine specialist access.
