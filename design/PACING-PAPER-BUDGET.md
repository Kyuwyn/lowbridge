# Early pacing paper budget: first worked allocation

18 October 2026. **Every duration, price allowance, numerical home benefit, age conversion, and modeled visit setting in this document is provisional.** Q122 accepts the normal 8-12-hour rhythm between meaningful decisions; Q120-Q121 accept one-for-one stored time with prompt spending. Those rules constrain this exercise. They do not approve the numbers below. See the [pacing draft](PACING-DRAFT.md) and [ADR 0019](../docs/architecture/adr-0019-early-campaign-pacing.md) for accepted targets.

This is an allocation to the existing Body → Iron Marrow → Jade Body example, not a measured playthrough or proof that other allocations cannot meet the earlier dates. It replaces the unallocated 176-hour envelope **as the current calculation example only**. Q123 accepts roughly 3-5 real days for both example journeys, superseding the earlier Cloudwell dates without approving the individual numerical inputs here. No implementation is authorized.

The longest individual objective is 12 hours. Completed teaching, a new finding, a chosen purchase, a practice limit, or an existing trial decision supplies the next opportunity to choose. Short related observations can remain one outing; the tables do not require a separate click for every observation or every result. They add no generic readiness quota or extra examination.

In the worked visit model, 10-minute visits two or three times daily finish the focused journey after about **3.2-3.5 elapsed days** and the curious journey after about **3.4-4.5 days**. These results informed Q123's accepted compact 3-5-day direction, replacing the former days 7-10 and 10-14 targets. These are outputs of the chosen assumptions, not forecasts of player behavior. Actual prices, learning durations, interface effort, and narrative presentation could change them.

A slow interface or unspent bank can make a calendar longer without adding cultivation. They should not be used to force the old targets. Conversely, these selected timers do not prove the targets impossible. Keeping the original dates may warrant additional substantial content or explicitly different commitments, with any affected readiness agreements revisited.

## Allocation conventions

- Durations mean normal activity hours, including progress supplied by the bank. They are not mandatory real-time waits once stored time is available.
- Purchase shortfalls are provisionally priced in hours of available paid work. Currency units, prices, earnings rates, and effects of alternative jobs remain open. Owned equipment is reused. These allowances must be replaced with an actual economic calculation later.
- Baseline practice times are candidate net requirements for the channel-route character, who already owns its useful practice arrangement. Its exact numerical effect is not separately selected here. The curious study-space saving below is an additional explicit sensitivity assumption, not a remote bonus to basin activities.
- A row may combine a short journey and its intended investigation when they are one outing. It must not also be charged in the local-travel allowance. Ordinary known return travel is distinct from learning the route again.
- Choosing a class and starting its prepared breakthrough require deliberate input. The practice demonstration is included in completing the taught objective; it is not a later exam.
- Some short actions represent repeated field observations over fictional days. Longer study or practice represents sustained work. The candidate age scale needs a fiction review: especially the length of study from short notes and of slow Cloudwell settling.

## Visit-model assumptions and sensitivity

A small arithmetic model advances one active objective until it completes, banks unused real time, and lets a scheduled visit select the next known objective and explicitly spend bank. It preserves every objective boundary and the Cloudwell pause. It does not choose actions while absent.

Reference assumptions for this calculation:

- A fresh start, no preloaded bank, and an initial 30-minute onboarding visit.
- Two evenly spaced visits every 12 hours or three every 8 hours. These are simplified schedules, not recommendations for sleeping or attending the game.
- Ten minutes per later visit. Selecting an objective and choosing to spend bank each receive a one-minute allowance for reading/decision/control use. These are placeholders, not measured interface effort or ten distinct strategic choices per visit.
- Actual normal activity proceeds during the visit too. A short objective may finish while the player is still present. Prompt spending stops at completion or the next meaningful boundary, and another spend requires another modeled decision.
- Both 48- and 72-hour bank caps were checked. Neither changes the ten-minute results because neither run loses time at those caps. This does not select a cap for release.
- Follow the authored comparison route with all relevant leads understood. There is no claim that a new player will take exactly these actions or read at this pace.

| Ten-minute visit schedule | Focused foundation | Focused breakthrough 2 | Focused breakthrough 3 | Focused Cloudwell | Curious Cloudwell |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Every 12 hours | 24.07 h | 36.03 h | 48.17 h | 84.03 h / 3.50 days | 108.07 h / 4.50 days |
| Every 8 hours | 16.07 h | 32.03 h | 48.13 h | 76.10 h / 3.17 days | 80.45 h / 3.35 days |

The twice-daily foundation result is just beyond 24 hours: the opening's interaction burden still needs work to robustly meet the first-day target. Reducing barriers in the interface must preserve meaningful choices. With fifteen-minute visits, foundation occurs around 8.23-12.23 hours in this model and Cloudwell around 3.17 days focused or 3.35-3.50 days curious.

Five-minute visits are materially different under the same one-minute decision assumptions. With a 48-hour cap, the focused run takes approximately 3.67-5.50 days and the curious run 5.33-7.50 days; some runs lose stored time at the cap. Foundation can slip to 24-36 hours. Those delays expose sensitivity to visit capacity, not evidence that the original calendar targets have been successfully met. Do not balance the game around slow control use or storage losses.

For each run, normal activity completed equals the sum of the route's durations, and real elapsed time equals completed activity plus remaining bank plus cap losses. These arithmetic checks passed. This verifies the model's accounting, not the game's enjoyment, accessibility, economy, or real-world pacing.

## Fictional time and reserve check

The earlier candidate of one fictional month per activity hour would yield only 6.34 focused years with this smaller workload. That falls short of Q117's accepted 10-20-year span. A **new, unaccepted calculation candidate** is **one ordinary activity hour = one sixth of a fictional year**, or two nominal months. It gives 12.68 focused years and 13.41 curious years, with no additional real waiting. The common scale, efficiency rule, and bank age costs remain unchanged. Calendar convention, display units, and the final rate remain open.

|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Foundation | 19.33 | 19.33 | Optional quiet corner: earning shortfall and setup | 1.10 | 19.52 |
| Breakthrough 2 | 22.73 | 23.37 | Try the Earth introduction, with existing basic field equipment | 0.50 | 23.63 |
| Breakthrough 3 | 24.82 | 26.62 | Collector's specimen-case commission, including a modest material shortfall | 0.35 | 26.94 |
| Breakthrough 4 | 30.68 | 31.41 | One chosen ordinary gathering objective at the opened survey-spur site, including its local trip | 0.25 | 31.78 |

These reserve activities come from existing optional content. Their values are illustrative, not new compulsory tasks. Count reserve age cumulatively. To be conservative, this bound does not subtract quiet-corner benefits, reuse savings on the later study-space investment, or commission earnings that could reduce subsequent paid work. Playing those reserves should credit such overlaps; this table must not become a recipe that charges them twice.

The largest resulting age is under 32, leaving over 48 years even against the unextended limit of 80. Thus **this numerical example** has ample room for Q116's minimum reserve without optional longevity or the faster draught. The calculation does not remove the accepted breakthrough extensions or set their amounts. It does not prove all class histories, arbitrary detours, or repeated withdrawals viable.
