## Accepted three-stage interaction

Q77 accepts initial setup followed by three stages, with **one meaningful decision pause midway**. Exact requirements, materials, effects, and costs remain open.

Before beginning, the player selects an eligible intended class, an understood approach, and suitable preparation. The plan explains its continuation choices and what they consume. A workable setup includes a known means of stopping further intake, so the slower response is available without a fourth-breakthrough ability. Specific site options and exact requirements within the accepted readiness package remain open.

| Stage | What happens | Player control and outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Admit a measured portion** | Execute the chosen approach to draw in a controlled amount of concentrated qi. As it circulates, its rhythm differs from the cultivator's established method; continuing to admit more would deepen the mismatch. | Choose the initial setup, then let the known activity run. Observation reveals the mismatch and pauses before further intake. |
| **2. Bring the borrowed qi into rhythm** | Stop fresh intake and decide how to bring the existing portion under control. | Choose slower settling, a compatible prepared response, or withdrawal. The choice determines the remaining time and used preparations. |
| **3. Complete circulation and release** | Carry out the selected response and release the excess in a controlled way. | The activity runs to completion automatically. Successful completion earns the breakthrough and intended class; there is no extra confirmation that adds no decision. |

### The mid-trial decision

- **Settle the portion gradually.** Isolate fresh intake and work through established circulation or controlled applications at a slower pace. This takes more remaining activity time and lifespan, but consumes no additional preparation at this decision beyond resources already used by the approach.
- **Use a prepared response.** Isolate intake and apply a compatible, already-understood preparation or prepared configuration. It shortens the remaining work while consuming or expending the stated repeatable preparation. The required support must actually be available for the attempt; an unavailable response explains what is missing.
- **Withdraw.** Release the held qi without completing the transformation and end the attempt. Keep findings and established advancement; elapsed time and preparation already used remain spent.

The interface shows remaining activity time, lifespan cost, and consumed preparation before a continuation is chosen. Exact values are still open. Both continuation choices can succeed when their requirements are met. Existing lifespan limits and safety rules still apply; an adequate technique does not supply additional life. Deterministic success is compatible with a trial whose decisions concern preparation and resource use, and there is no separate random failure roll.

The pause does not consume lifespan or let the trial expire. Unused real time follows the accepted time-bank rules. An instruction to spend banked time ends at this meaningful pause; the player can enable it again for the selected continuation. A separate pressure or stability meter is not needed for this authored interaction.
