ADR 0018: First defining trial at breakthrough 4

Status: Accepted timing, advancement relationship, natural qi convergence premise, three shared approach families, three-stage interaction with one mid-trial decision, practical common readiness, the first outer-basin observation and practice-site finding, the introductory control-teaching exchange, early gentle-control practice for every starter, a deeper shared convergence site, its early investigative expedition, five preparations based on existing capabilities, a shared field archive, the arrangement and measured-pulse control demonstrations, a conditioning dose per attempt, reinforced and grounded preparation, reusable setup for the arrangement and pulse routes, and a shared optional settling draught; exact requirements, support effects, and costs remain open. Date: 16 October 2026; updated 18 October 2026. Basis: explicit answers Q74-Q78 and Q96-Q107, with regional-access and arrival cross-references. Q79's broad class preparation and benefit packages are recorded separately in ADR 0017.

Breakthrough 4 introduces the campaign's first defining trial. The first three breakthroughs remain prepared milestones. Once ready for an eligible intended class, the player chooses and carries out a suitable approach using capabilities available before this advancement. Success earns the breakthrough and the new class; neither its mastery contribution nor its new abilities can be prerequisites for the trial that awards it.

The accepted trial rules continue to apply:

- Outcomes follow preparation and choices, without a separate random failure roll.
- Every valid history and intended class needs a viable noncombat approach and independently accessible required teaching and materials.
- An unsuccessful attempt with lifespan remaining costs time and repeatable preparation while retaining established advancement and discoveries.
- Lifespan exhaustion instead uses the existing exact-state checkpoint retry and current time-bank rules.
- One primary activity, objectives, meaningful decision pauses, knowledge-limited practice, and ordinary lifespan accounting remain in force.

Q75 selects a natural qi convergence in the next connected region as the shared trial premise, provisionally called the Cloudwell. The cultivator tempers their existing foundation by admitting concentrated qi, circulating a controlled amount, and releasing the excess. Different existing builds can develop suitable approaches. The player begins when prepared; there is no real-time deadline. Specific sites, techniques, and costs remain open.

Q76 accepts three shared approaches:

- **Regulate from within:** control the intake through internal circulation.
- **Shape the surrounding flow:** regulate incoming qi through an external arrangement.
- **Work in measured pulses:** guide manageable portions through precise applications.

Availability depends on learned capabilities and suitable preparation. Compatible techniques, equipment, and conditioning preparations can support an approach. Different class histories can share it, and it need not match the intended class. This settles the shared approach structure; exact capability requirements, supported combinations, effects, and costs remain open.

Q77 accepts three stages with one mid-trial decision pause:

1. The chosen approach admits a controlled portion of qi. Its rhythm conflicts with the cultivator's established circulation, and the activity pauses before further intake.
2. The player chooses slower settling, a compatible prepared response, or withdrawal. Slower settling takes more remaining time and lifespan without extra preparation at this choice. A prepared response reduces the remaining time while expending its stated preparation. Withdrawal retains findings and advancement but leaves elapsed time and used preparation spent.
3. The selected continuation completes circulation and release automatically. Success earns the breakthrough and intended class.

Both continuation options can succeed when their requirements are satisfied. The pause has no deadline. Exact requirements, materials, effects, activity durations, and lifespan costs remain open. Specific response examples and the full readiness package are not settled by this structure.

Q78 accepts practical common readiness alongside preparation for the intended class:

1. Understand one usable Cloudwell site.
2. Demonstrate at gentle exposure that the chosen existing method can admit a small amount of qi, stop further intake, and release it.
3. Have whatever basic equipment, arrangements, or materials the approach actually requires.

Qualifying earlier work can satisfy both class preparation and shared trial readiness. Knowledge and demonstrated control survive ordinary retries; only missing knowledge, setup, or consumed preparation needs replacing. Exact-state checkpoint retries are unchanged. The faster mid-trial response remains optional. There is no additional broad milestone count for breakthrough 4; the three-of-five package remains specific to breakthrough 3. Exact capability requirements, equipment, materials, and costs remain open.

These decisions do not require a combat encounter or sect examination or approve a new resource meter. The later trial schedule remains open.

Q92 accepts regional entry before breakthrough 3, as recorded in [ADR 0012](adr-0012-second-breakthrough-arc.md). Early arrival grants no trial readiness by itself. Actual qualifying observations and preparation still receive credit; breakthrough 3 and all intended-class and trial requirements remain necessary before breakthrough 4.

Q93 accepts arrival at a market settlement on the outer edge of the Cloudwell basin, with useful visitor work/trade, an accessible observation lead, and an independent contact pointing toward teaching and gentle preparation sites. These leads are optional; receiving them does not complete trial readiness. See [ADR 0012](adr-0012-second-breakthrough-arc.md) and the [regional design](../../design/FOURTH-BREAKTHROUGH-DRAFT.md).

Q96 accepts the first outer-basin observation: visible mist is an unreliable guide to qi. Mist can linger over a stone shelf after qi recedes while weak flow continues through an exposed outward channel. Comparing the observations establishes the local pattern and a reachable place for gentle practice once a suitable method and preparation are available. Any starting class can investigate with basic qi perception. The finding supplies site knowledge without demonstrating intake control or establishing full-trial readiness. Qualifying knowledge counts later without repeating the investigation. Exact method compatibility, scenes, requirements, and durations remain open.

Q97 accepts the field-report exchange with the independent basin practitioner, who keeps local flow records. Reporting the accepted mist-and-qi findings earns access to introductory teaching for gentle intake, stopping, and release through a suitable method. Already-completed observations count. If the cultivator cannot yet perform the practice, the earned teaching remains available without another survey, payment, or favor. Basic directions and teaching leads are available on meeting. Reporting and instruction do not themselves demonstrate practical control, award mastery, or establish full-trial readiness; equivalent instruction learned elsewhere counts. Exact lessons, method compatibility, character details, and durations remain open.

Q98 accepts that every starting class can complete the first gentle-control exercise before breakthrough 3, using the shared foundation and earned introductory lesson. One practice objective admits a small portion from the shelf’s weak flow, stops intake, and releases it beside the understood outlet. Completion earns credit for that internal method’s basic-control demonstration when used later. A different arrangement or measured-pulse method requires only different control steps. The exercise grants no class or mastery; full-trial capacity, trial-site knowledge, setup, intended-class preparation, and the required breakthroughs remain separate. Exact thresholds, supplies, and durations remain open.

Q99 accepts a shared convergence site farther inside the same basin for the full trial, separate from the outer practice shelf. Stronger qi flows meet there, giving exploration a further destination and supporting all three trial approaches with suitable preparation. Earlier observations and matching control demonstrations retain their credit; further study addresses only unknown conditions at the new site. Q100 below establishes early investigative access; exact equipment and durations remain open. This location decision adds no automatic advancement gate for exploration and requires no relocation.

Q100 accepts a short, optional expedition from the settlement to sheltered observation points at the deeper convergence before breakthrough 3. Every starting class can investigate with basic qi perception and suitable ordinary preparation. Observation reveals two incoming flows strengthening at different points in their cycles, while an outward channel continues carrying qi away; comparison identifies possible intake positions and a reliable release direction. The finding supplies useful site knowledge without establishing full-trial capacity, and earlier matching control demonstrations still count. Once explored, the route supports ordinary return travel with normal time and lifespan costs. Exact equipment, durations, and method-specific preparation remain open.

Q101 accepts five preparation directions for concentrated exposure: Iron Marrow adapts reinforced circulation; Mountain Vessel supports circulation through a compatible stationary ground connection; Pill Refiner learns appropriate bodily conditioning at Alchemy 1, usable with shared circulation from any starting class; Earth Array prepares a manually maintained intake-and-diversion layout; and Spirit Blade learns a specific blade-guided method using separate precise applications with stops and resets. These feed into the three shared trial approaches. Class names identify natural capability sources, not exclusive current-class gates; retained abilities and suitable equivalent teaching count. The player prepares one workable approach, with earlier matching demonstrations credited. Necessary conditioning supports the slower continuation too, and the faster response remains optional. Exact lessons, mechanisms, supplies, costs, and durations remain open.

Q102 accepts a shared field archive kept by the independent basin practitioner, with access included in the established introductory teaching exchange. Earlier cultivators contributed circulation notes, stationary-ground-connection accounts, intake-layout diagrams, guided-pulse instructions, and a suitable Alchemy-1 conditioning recipe. Study the guide needed for the chosen method; deeper convergence findings help apply it to the site, and equivalent prior instruction and observations count. Access requires no further favor or fee and can be earned early. Practical use still requires appropriate capabilities, demonstrated control, and supplies. Preparation for the intended fourth-breakthrough class remains separate. Exact texts, recipe details, and study durations remain open.

Q103 accepts two gentle-control demonstrations. The arrangement admits weak flow through a small temporary layout; the cultivator physically adjusts its control point to divert fresh flow toward the outlet, closes intake, then circulates and releases the admitted portion. The measured-pulse method admits a thin portion from an understood side-current, uses the specifically taught projected blade cut to separate it from its feeding thread, closes intake, then circulates and releases before resetting. Each is one ordinary practice objective at the outer shelf or another suitable understood site, using available abilities and relevant teaching. Only the chosen method's missing demonstration is required, qualifying earlier work counts, and the internal-circulation exercise retains its credit. These establish basic control; full trial preparation and intended-class readiness remain necessary. Exact equipment, quantities, and durations remain open.

Q104 accepts one prepared dose for each attempt using the conditioning-supported approach. The archive's Alchemy-1 recipe combines obtainable herbs and a simple mineral ingredient in an ordinary vessel. Ingredients can be bought or gathered without another class or a home upgrade. One normal crafting objective produces usable preparation, and the first successful batch can supply the trial. A dose is consumed when intake begins, supporting the whole attempt including slower settling and the decision pause. An ordinary withdrawal afterward leaves that dose spent; unused doses remain available. It supplies this approach's necessary conditioning, separately from any optional faster response. The requirement follows the selected approach, not every character with Pill Refiner. Exact ingredients, quantities, batch yield, costs, and durations remain open. Existing retry and checkpoint rules still apply.

Q105 accepts the remaining preparation for reinforced and grounded internal circulation. The reinforced route combines earned bodily reinforcement with the archive adaptation, which teaches its use during intake, stopping, circulation, and release. Existing basic-control credit and usable-site knowledge count, with no additional generic training test or mandatory consumable. The grounded route uses archive guidance and the existing stationary-connection ability to identify a compatible trial position and establish the connection, as one site-preparation objective where equivalent survey and alignment are missing. Later attempts retain that knowledge and re-establish the connection only as needed. The connection supports the entire attempt, including slower settling and the decision pause. Both routes still require intended-class preparation and all other agreed trial conditions. Exact equipment, costs, and durations remain open.

Q106 accepts reusable setup for the arrangement and measured-pulse routes at the full trial site. The arrangement fits the taught layout to known currents using ordinary reusable field markers, with components and reachable controls placed before intake and active tending during the attempt. The pulse route uses a suitable ordinary blade, an accessible narrow feeding thread identified from archive guidance and site observations, and a stable position where the thread is perceptible and within demonstrated range. Suitable existing equipment, earlier knowledge, and gentle demonstrations count; only missing setup needs restoration on later attempts. The arrangement requires active tending, and the blade route needs no separate formation or special spirit weapon. Both support slower settling without mandatory additional consumables. Intended-class readiness still applies. Exact equipment, setup time, costs, and durations remain open.

Q107 accepts one shared settling draught for the optional faster continuation across the three trial approaches. Carry a dose into the attempt; after intake stops, choosing the faster response consumes it to help existing circulation settle and release the held qi sooner while the chosen technique continues as required. Every supported history can buy and use it before breakthrough 4; making it personally is optional. The settling draught is separate from the baseline conditioning dose: conditioning supports the exposure approach, while settling shortens the remaining work. Both can serve their distinct purposes in one attempt. Choosing slower settling or withdrawing at the pause leaves the draught unused. The choice shows remaining time, lifespan cost, and the dose spent. Exact seller, recipe, price, and time saved remain open.
