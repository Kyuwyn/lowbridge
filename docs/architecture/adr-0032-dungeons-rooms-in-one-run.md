ADR 0032: Dungeons: rooms in one run

Status: Accepted semantics for the two basin-road dungeons, the surveyors' adit and the Reedmarket cistern. Every room length, drain, foe and payout is content and provisional. Date: 28 October 2026. Basis: the owner's decisions of 28 October (two dungeons, the adit mid-chapter and Strain-heavy, the cistern after arrival and Focus-heavy, first clears permanent, repeats paying materials and knowledge, the adit as a seventh Second Direction pointer with `required` still 4, run rules copying Arcanum rule 27); [ADR 0022](adr-0022-chore-tempo.md) (one slot, a waiting list, `pause` and resumption), [ADR 0026](adr-0026-combat-exchanges.md) (fights, the exact win chance, a fight cannot be left halfway), [ADR 0027](adr-0027-locks-at-payment-and-stops.md) (locks at payment, a paid run resumes without a second bill), [ADR 0028](adr-0028-ten-minute-foundation.md) (the ten-minute rule), [ADR 0031](adr-0031-basin-road-systems.md) (Focus, foe techniques), `research/arcanum-reference/08-adventure-and-combat.md` and `12-design-rules.md` rules 1, 2, 7, 10, 12, 22, 23, 27, 28, 29, 30, 36, 70.

**Context.** The basin road had one fight (the pair at the spur) and nine outings, each a single timed run. The reference's adventure layer is a series of rooms inside one slot activity: rule 27 (*copy*), **adventures are slot activities with floors; leaving keeps progress; death costs time only; bosses reset**, and rule 30 (*copy*), **locales are built from a shared encounter pool**. ADR 0026 copied rule 27 "except for floors" and left open "dungeons of several fights and how Life carries between them". The owner asked for dungeons on the basin road "using Arcanum as a base": a series of rooms, each a test or a fight, with a boost or reward at the end, usually repeatable. The two chosen close two things the chapter already promised: the adit beyond the reopened spur, whose survey marks the spur's own event mentions, and the cistern under Reedmarket, which the `cistern_leaf` lead named and nothing yet let the player walk.

**Decision.**

1. **A dungeon is a `site` in the `Dungeons` group carrying `rooms`, nothing else of a run.** A room is `{ name, length, strain?, need?, needtext?, result?, encounter? }`. The record keeps its own `require`, `cost` (the entry bill), `result`, `at`, `every`, `mod`, `events` and `max`; its `length` is the sum of its rooms and is derived by the engine, never authored (`withRoomLengths` in `src/engine.ts`), so everything that treats a timed record as a timed record (the slot, the waiting list, `paid`, `exp`, the bank, saves) treats a dungeon the same way with no new state. The validator refuses `length`, `strain`, `run`, `encounter`, `effect`, `train`, `fill`, `hobby`, `autoStart`, `lasts`, `attune` and `boost` on a dungeon, a `Dungeons` record without rooms, fewer than two rooms, a room over `TIMED_CEILING` (180 s), rooms over `DUNGEON_CEILING` (600 s) together, a door without `needtext`, and any `mod`/`perLevel`/`at`/`every`/`held` path that writes a dungeon's `length` (rule 1 kept: one vocabulary, one new field).

2. **Rooms run in order inside the one run, and each pays as it is cleared.** Progress through the run is progress through the rooms: a room spans `[start, end)` in run seconds. Each second drains the current room's `strain` (as a record's `strain` drains); when progress reaches a room's end the overshoot is dropped (a second belongs to one room), the room's `result` is paid flat (the run's own `result` keeps the yield rule of ADR 0022), and "`<dungeon>: <room> is behind you (n of N)`" is logged. The last room's end completes the run as any site's: `reps`, `result` with yield, `at`/`every`, the first completion's `mod`, and the entry bill falls due again for a repeat. Rooms are inline on the dungeon, not a shared pool (see divergences).

3. **Doors.** A room is entered only through its door, checked when the run starts at that room and again between rooms, every visit: its `need` (`needtext` says what is missing, rule 2), Life of at least one whole point when the room holds a fight (ADR 0026's wording and "ready in" estimate), and the reserve rule of ADR 0026 extended to **every** room kind: each drained gauge must hold, after its own regeneration, the whole units the rest of *that room* costs (`reserveNeeded` and the waiting list's `recovered` both read the room, not the run). A shut door between rooms pauses the run with "`<room> is behind you; <problem>`": the dungeon parks in the waiting list with its `exp` at the next room's start, and resumes by itself once the door opens (the gauge regenerated, the need met), as any parked pursuit does (ADR 0022). A gauge that still runs dry mid-room pauses through the ordinary `unsustained` rule and resumes mid-room. Nothing resets a room behind you.

4. **Fights per room (rule 27 copied).** A fight room enters a fight on its first second with the stats and whole Life held then, swings on the Speed clocks of ADR 0026 for at most the room's `length`, cannot be stopped or replaced while undecided, and is saved mid-fight with its room (`fight.room`, save v7 unchanged in shape: an optional field). A decided fight ends its room then and there. A **won** room pays `encounter.success` and its `result`. A **lost** room (Life out, or the foe outlasting the room) ends the outing: the run leaves the slot with the loss text as the last stop, the run's progress returns to **that room's start**, `paid` stays true, and the rooms behind stay walked; starting the dungeon again resumes at the lost room's door with no second entry bill (ADR 0027). Losing never locks anything and never waits. Life is the live store and carries between rooms with its ordinary regeneration only; nothing inside a dungeon heals (this settles ADR 0026's open "how Life carries").

5. **First clears are permanent; repeats pay materials and knowledge.** The first walk to the last room fires an event that sets a flag (`tunnel_walked`, `cistern_found`) carrying a cap raise (+2 Strain, +2 Focus); the cistern's flag reveals the leaf (`cistern_slip_leaf`, `t_leaf`, Composure patches Focus's cap) and satisfies the `cistern_leaf` lead's new `need`. Every run pays the dungeon's `result` (experience toward two families) and the rooms' materials; `every: { "3": … }` adds pale root (the adit) or coin (the cistern). The adit's flag is the Second Direction's seventh pointer; `required` stays 4 of 7, so no route needs it and every route may count it (rule 12).

6. **Projection and presentation.** `fight(id, room?)` projects a dungeon's fight for the room you stand in or the room asked for, with the room's length as the limit; the tooltip gains a `rooms` block after `time` (one line per room: length, the fight's verdict, drains, the door, the loot, and "← you are here"), its `time` line reads "N rooms · mm:ss in all", and the `fight` block appears when the room you stand in holds one. The card sub-line reads "Room n of N: <name>" while standing in one; the Fight tab titles a room's fight "<dungeon> · <room>" with the room's limit. No new panel and no new `kind`.

7. **Pacing witness.** `bun run validate` requires every dungeon to be cleared (`reps >= 1`) on some played route and witnesses both on all ten deep routes after Reedmarket (`scripts/routes.ts`); the route runner waits at a shut door or for Life after a loss, as a player would. The Second Direction and arrival bands are unchanged by the dungeons, which the deep routes walk after arriving.

**Divergences from *copy* rules, with reasons.**

- **Rule 30 (locales from a shared encounter pool): rooms are inline on the dungeon, not a pool of room records.** Reason: two dungeons of four rooms do not justify a second record type and the cross-references it would need; the vocabulary grows by one field. A pool can be extracted when a third dungeon shares a room.
- **Rule 27 is copied with one narrowing.** Leaving keeps progress; a lost fight resets the room; nothing behind resets. The reference's bosses reset the whole adventure on leaving; Lowbridge's fights cannot be left while undecided (ADR 0026), so a boss that resets on leaving has no second to reset in. "Death costs time only" is kept as written: a loss costs the room's time and nothing else of what was walked; the entry bill is the preparation of ADR 0026 and it is not paid again.
- **Rule 22 (distance as a bought axis): not taken up.** The dungeons are gated by the chapter's leads and flags (the spur reopened, Reedmarket reached), not by a distance resource; the basin road has no mounts and ADR 0031 did not add any.
- **Rule 29 (five stresses as HP)** stays as ADR 0031 settled it: Strain and Focus are the two gauges, and the rooms draw on them in different proportions (the adit mostly Strain, the cistern mostly Focus).
- **Rule 28 (quickslots as the active layer)** stays as ADR 0031 settled it: a room's fight is swung out on what is held at entry.
- **Rule 36 (bestiary / travelogue): not taken up.** The journal's "is behind you" lines and the foe's line in the tooltip are the record; a room list that unlocks entry by entry is for a slice with more than two dungeons.
- **Rule 23 and 70 are copied.** A dungeon is one slot activity with one bill; its `desc` names every room, drain, door, payout and the loss rule in plain words.

**Consequences.** `src/types.ts` gains `Room`, `GameRecord.rooms` and `FightState.room`; `TOOLTIP_BLOCKS` gains `rooms` between `time` and `fight`. The engine gains room position helpers (`roomAt`, `encounterOf`, `withRoomLengths`, `roomsLength`), doors, per-room fight entry, settlement and projection, and the room-clear step in the timed tick; `fight(id, lifeHeld?)`'s unused second argument becomes the room. Persistence decodes `fight.room` and derives dungeon lengths before reading a save. The validator admits `rooms` with the grammar above and the route-clear witness; the route runner reads records as the engine does and waits at doors. The interface gains the Dungeons group in the Tasks tab, the rooms block and the room-aware card line and Fight tab. Content gains `survey_tunnel`, `reedmarket_cistern`, `tunnel_walked`, `cistern_found`, `cistern_slip_leaf`, the seventh pointer and the cistern lead's `need`; the chapter is 219 records. Tests cover room order and payout, a door halt and its bill-free resumption, a lost room's reset, first-clear against repeat payout, per-room projection and the tooltip, the mid-fight save with its room, the grammar, and the deep routes clearing both. One pre-existing engine fact is sharper here: the slot drains `run`/`strain` undiscounted while reserves and tooltips read them discounted (`src/engine.ts` `tick` against `drain`), so a Focus-discounted room can run a fraction short and pause mid-room; it resumes, and the routes still clear.

**Supersessions.** Supersedes ADR 0026's divergence "a fight is a single timed run, not a multi-floor site" with "a fight is a single timed run, or one room of a dungeon". Settles ADR 0026's open "dungeons of several fights and how Life carries between them" (§4) and "encounter pools" (as inline rooms, for this slice). Amends the chapter-two document's groups and record tables.

Still open: a shared room pool once a third dungeon shares a room; a boss room that resets rooms behind it on a loss (rule 27's clash with ADR 0026); a per-room `cost` beyond the drains; healing inside a dungeon (a rest room, a draught taken between rooms); a room list that unlocks entry by entry (rule 36); and the balance of every figure above.
