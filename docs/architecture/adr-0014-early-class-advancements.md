ADR 0014: Early class choices and major breakthroughs

Status: Accepted timing and cumulative structure for breakthroughs 2-4, four starting directions, and the first dedicated alchemy class at breakthrough 3; names and detailed mechanics remain in design. Date: 16 October 2026. Basis: explicit answers Q64-Q65, following the requested Arcanum class/mastery study and early cultivation class-map sketch.

The first major breakthrough establishes the shared foundation without selecting a class. Each of major breakthroughs 2, 3, and 4 combines realm advancement with one lasting addition to the character's class history. Earlier acquired classes remain part of that history; advancing adds to the build rather than replacing its previous identity and benefits.

This settles the schedule of the first three class choices. It does not automatically assign a class choice to every remaining breakthrough or to final ascension. The eight-breakthrough campaign target remains in place.

Q65 selects four directions for the first class choice at breakthrough 2: body cultivation, sword cultivation, earth attunement, and formations. The first dedicated alchemy class becomes available at breakthrough 3, alongside that stage's other class choices. This does not prohibit earlier alchemy discoveries, introductory practices, or useful preparations, and does not require the player to choose alchemy.

The [early class map](../../design/CULTIVATION-CLASS-MAP.md) illustrates those starting directions and later specializations and hybrids. Q64-Q65 do not approve the exact class names, the six proposed mastery fields or assignments, contribution counts, later class roster, requirements, benefits, costs, or durations. Teachings, discoveries, and practice remain part of becoming ready; their concrete relationship to each class needs design.

Subsequent Q66 accepts the +1-per-listed-mastery counting rule and the four starting assignments in [ADR 0015](adr-0015-cumulative-class-masteries.md). Later class assignments and requirements remain open.

The former single-path replacement/retraining model is no longer the structure for ordinary advancement through these three choices. Whether and how a player may revise an earlier class choice remains open. Exact checkpoint capture timing also remains open; this decision does not grant an additional respec or retry benefit.

The accepted ridge arc still places the prepared second breakthrough before crossing into the waystation. Its recovered teachings, introductions, and capability applications must be developed for the four starting directions. The first substantial lead still points toward another connected region, with relocation separate. Optional combat and sect membership, knowledge-limited practice, and the existing lifespan/time-bank rules remain in force.

See the [source study](../../research/arcanum-study-2026-10-16/CLASS-MASTERY-STUDY.md), [campaign progression decision](adr-0005-campaign-progression.md), and [ridge arc decision](adr-0012-second-breakthrough-arc.md). Earlier three-path records remain design history where they conflict with this accepted early advancement structure.
