ADR 0016: Third-breakthrough classes and common readiness

Status: Accepted candidate roster, mastery requirements, contributions, three-of-five common milestone package, prepared-milestone breakthrough, broad preparation/benefit packages, the teaching-source mix, recovery/study or useful-task acquisition, the two practitioner tasks, five first applications of acquired class benefits, three recovered-teaching objectives, and five practical demonstrations; final names, exact documents and characters, recovery/study details and task parameters, detailed recipes, numerical requirements and effects, costs, and durations remain open. Date: 16 October 2026; updated 17 October 2026. Basis: explicit answers Q67-Q70 and Q86-Q91, plus the approval, “These look good, let's use them,” of the five preparation-and-benefit packages.

The third major breakthrough adds one of the following five classes to the existing history. Names identify working candidates and are not final lore names. Requirements inspect mastery already earned before choosing; each listed contribution adds +1.

| Working class | Earlier mastery required | Contributions |
|---|---|---|
| Iron Marrow | Body ≥1 | Body +1 |
| Mountain Vessel | Body ≥1 or Earth ≥1 | Body +1, Earth +1 |
| Spirit Blade | Sword ≥1 or Spirit ≥1 | Sword +1, Spirit +1 |
| Earth Array | Earth ≥1 or Formation ≥1 | Earth +1, Formation +1 |
| Pill Refiner | No prior mastery required | Alchemy +1, Body +1 |

Pill Refiner is available from any starting class through accessible refining teaching and practical preparation. All five candidates require practical readiness beyond any mastery gate. Qualifying for a candidate does not force the choice or grant its benefits in advance. Acquiring one class retains the earlier class and its benefits.

Q68 accepts common readiness through a small selection of useful milestones from practice, investigation, home development, or crafting/equipment, alongside preparation for the chosen class. The player need not complete every milestone or one in every category. The same accomplishment may satisfy class preparation and common readiness where appropriate. These are explicit conditions, not spendable points.

Q69 makes the third breakthrough a prepared milestone. Once the intended class's preparation and the selected common readiness milestones are complete, a deliberate attempt succeeds. There is no additional defining trial at this transition. Q74 subsequently places the first defining trial at breakthrough 4; later placements remain open.

After Q69, the user accepted the five preparation-and-benefit packages presented for review:

- Iron Marrow develops taught conditioning and circulation under ordinary loads, using modest training equipment; advancement improves reinforcement and strenuous physical work.
- Mountain Vessel learns to connect circulation with ground currents, including missing introductory skills; advancement grants sustained stability at suitable terrain, modest bodily development, and further Earth opportunities.
- Spirit Blade learns controlled force at contact using blade instruction and suitable equipment; advancement projects precise force beyond contact for practical operations and optional combat.
- Earth Array combines ground sensing, external arrangements, a surveyed site, and a simple practice layout; advancement supports more capable formations using terrain and local qi, including home and expedition uses.
- Pill Refiner learns extraction and controlled heating with an ordinary vessel and accessible ingredients; advancement enables useful bodily conditioning preparations and deeper refining.

Preparation must be achievable before acquiring the class. Pill Refiner's introductory teaching and ingredients are accessible from every starting class. Iron Marrow has the greater direct physical advantage; Mountain Vessel gains terrain-dependent possibilities. Earth Array deepens formation practice, and home arrangements use existing residence capacity. Exact teachings, item identities, recipes, numerical thresholds and effects, prices, and durations still need design.

Q70 accepts three of these five shared milestones, alongside all preparation for the intended class:

1. **A reliable method:** demonstrate a taught technique reliably. Suitable class preparation can satisfy this directly.
2. **The returning chime:** explain the waystation chime's delayed qi pulse through observation and a controlled test of its old relay connection.
3. **A working study space:** establish or adapt a useful home study/practice arrangement and demonstrate its benefit.
4. **Reopen the survey spur:** use suitable equipment to restore and traverse a damaged side route, earning useful access and retaining the equipment.
5. **A useful commission:** repair and fit a collector's specimen case, demonstrate that it protects its contents, and deliver it for payment and a reusable recipe.

Each milestone counts once; qualifying earlier work counts. Class preparation may also complete the technique milestone, so the usual route is that preparation plus two other projects. The player need not complete every category. Existing investments can supply requirements without replacement. Qualifying for three milestones does not automatically select a class or trigger the breakthrough.

The [third-breakthrough design](../../design/THIRD-BREAKTHROUGH-DRAFT.md) develops the accepted projects and overlap rules, with specific demonstrations and numerical pacing still subject to design and playtesting. These decisions apply to breakthrough 3; they do not prescribe the readiness structure for every later breakthrough. The fourth-breakthrough roster and broad class packages are subsequently accepted in [ADR 0017](adr-0017-fourth-breakthrough-choice-breadth.md), with the first defining trial in [ADR 0018](adr-0018-first-defining-trial.md).

See the [class map](../../design/CULTIVATION-CLASS-MAP.md), [early schedule](adr-0014-early-class-advancements.md), and [mastery counting rule](adr-0015-cumulative-class-masteries.md). Independent and noncombat progression, knowledge-limited practice, one primary activity, and the existing lifespan and time-bank rules remain in force.

Q86 accepts the third-breakthrough teaching-source mix: recovered conditioning notes for Iron Marrow; complementary circulation and ground-current records for Mountain Vessel; an independent blade practitioner for Spirit Blade; recovered survey and layout diagrams for Earth Array; and an independent local refiner for Pill Refiner. Sources are reachable around the village and waystation before completing common readiness, with missing basics available to eligible histories and no required sect, combat, relocation, or next-region travel. Obtaining teaching does not complete practical preparation. The refining introduction remains available for later Golden Cauldron entry. Exact characters, documents, recovery/study details, task quantities, costs, and durations remain open.

Q87 keeps focused recovery or study for the three recovered teachings and selects one bounded useful task for each independent practitioner in exchange for the instruction needed for class preparation. These tasks replace tuition. The teaching includes missing basics needed by eligible histories; already-acquired matching instruction counts. Tools, materials, and practical demonstrations remain separate, and obtaining teaching awards no milestone or mastery. Exact recovery/study details, characters, task quantities, resource costs, and durations remain open.

Q88 accepts the two practitioner tasks: record stray qi at marked practice-yard observation points for the blade practitioner, and obtain and deliver a guided batch of ordinary local herbs for the refiner, counting suitable stock already owned. Guidance and basic task tools are supplied; existing abilities suffice. Completion provides the full promised instruction without tuition or another favor, while practical preparation remains separate. Delivered herbs cannot also supply later practice, and neither exchange automatically completes a common milestone. Exact names, locations, quantities, and durations remain open.

Q89 accepts the five first applications: more efficient heavy hauling for Iron Marrow; a sustained stationary outcrop reading revealing a mineral-gathering patch for Mountain Vessel; a distant fastening release recovering supplies for Spirit Blade; a tended temporary field working improving survey or gathering for Earth Array; and a starter conditioning preparation shortening one suitable known practice objective for Pill Refiner. These are optional local uses of lasting benefits, available without another mandatory teaching or practice tier. Ordinary setup and materials still apply, required campaign materials have other sources, and exact scenes, effects, costs, and durations remain open.

Q90 accepts three bounded recovery/study objectives: reconstruct the complete conditioning sequence from annotated sheets for Iron Marrow; compare circulation and ground-current accounts for Mountain Vessel; and match a survey plan with its marker-layout overlay and interpret the supplied annotations for Earth Array. Each supplies the intended preparation teaching and eligible missing basics through ordinary study. Only the intended source is required, qualifying earlier study counts, and there is no separate puzzle interface or additional retrieval chain. Teaching alone completes no practical demonstration or common milestone. Exact document names, prose, placement, and durations remain open.

Q91 accepts the five practical demonstrations: maintain taught circulation through manageable load, posture, and exertion changes for Iron Marrow; hold a brief stationary ground connection under understood conditions for Mountain Vessel; make a controlled blade-contact cut without disturbing marked surrounding material for Spirit Blade; configure and manually maintain a small fixed layout through taught adjustments for Earth Array; or produce a usable beginner extract with an ordinary vessel and controlled heating for Pill Refiner. Each uses capabilities available before acquisition. The intended demonstration completes both that class-preparation step and the shared reliable-method milestone, without a duplicate examination. Qualifying earlier work counts; other teaching, mastery, equipment/material, and three-of-five requirements still apply. Exact thresholds, ingredients, and durations remain open.
