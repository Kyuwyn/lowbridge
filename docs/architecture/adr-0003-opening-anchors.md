ADR 0003: Opening anchors

Status: Accepted opening direction, rules, and activity package; details remain in design. Date: 16 October 2026. Basis: explicit answers in the opening-arc interview.

The campaign begins with an ordinary mortal and should establish the value of discovery early. Reliable mastery can develop over a longer arc while the world offers an early supernatural experience.

| Decision | Accepted direction |
|---|---|
| Starting setting | A village at the edge of the wilds, with local work and relationships and unfamiliar places within reach. |
| Initial access to cultivation | An incomplete method teaches qi perception but not stable cultivation. Investigating qi and learning to cultivate it reliably becomes the opening pursuit. |
| First unmistakable sign of qi | Early in the first session. The possibility of cultivation is established early; the full transformation remains a substantial pursuit. |
| Prior understanding | Cultivation is known but distant. Cultivators exist, while their practices and opportunities appear beyond an ordinary villager's reach. |
| Meaning of the village/home | A foothold of the protagonist's own: a place to establish independence and gradually build a place in the world. Familiar people may exist; inherited obligations are not the selected anchor. |
| First personal qi experience | Reveal hidden possibilities: familiar places acquire new details or opportunities ordinary perception missed. |
| Early expeditions | Investigation and practical obstacles lead: observation, terrain, hazards, and preparation. Combat can appear where it fits. |
| First major breakthrough and specialization | Establish a shared foundation that supports several paths. Path commitment follows later, once the player has discovered meaningful alternatives. |
| Investigation progress | Choose leads and approaches. Observations suggest possibilities; deciding what to investigate and preparing for it produces discoveries. |
| Source of reliable cultivation | Reconstruct a working practice through fieldwork, combining the fragment, observations, and practice. |
| First major breakthrough success | A prepared milestone: once the required preparations are satisfied, a deliberate attempt succeeds. The challenge is becoming ready. This does not settle later breakthrough or tribulation rules. |
| Expedition control | Choose a destination and purpose. The outing carries out known work between visits, pausing when a discovery creates a meaningful new choice. |
| Opening breadth | A small set of useful leads offers choices about investigation and preparation. Becoming ready for the first breakthrough does not require completing everything. |
| Activity concurrency | One primary activity at a time: work, practise, prepare, or explore. Home improvements provide benefits to the activity underway. |
| Home routine stopping | Stop at a chosen objective, such as earning toward a useful purchase or practising toward a known milestone, then pause for the player's next decision. |
| First major breakthrough pacing | Target a few short visits, roughly within the first day at ordinary play speed. This is a design target to validate, not measured pacing or a deadline. The first unmistakable qi experience still occurs early in the first session. |
| First arc direction | Keep village work and home improvements supporting two investigations: a damaged stream channel requiring practical preparation, and a sheltered hollow requiring better perception. Either can lead to the first breakthrough; the other remains useful afterward. Develop both investigations and their distinct rewards into the first playable arc. |
| Practice limits in the first arc | Knowledge sets clear limits. Practise up to what the fragment and current discoveries can teach; new understanding unlocks further growth or a new practice. |
| Ordinary expedition limits in the first arc | Preparation and access: equipment, learned techniques, and understood obstacles determine what can be attempted. Outings consume time and lifespan; the opening needs no additional energy or food meter. |
| Ordinary investigation setbacks before the first breakthrough | Pause with a useful finding: reveal the obstacle or missing understanding, preserve discoveries already made, and let the player choose how to prepare. The cost is time already spent. |
| Lifespan exhaustion before the first breakthrough | Retry from the opening starting state, restoring it exactly. Discoveries, skills, and purchases gained during the failed attempt are not retained. |
| First playable arc activity package | Use village errands, perception practice, field equipment, an optional home practice upgrade, both investigations, and method practice leading to the shared foundation. Develop their connections; exact names, costs, and durations remain open. |

These anchors do not establish a chosen-one history, the method's provenance, a fixed protagonist personality, a mandatory teacher, or a sect affiliation. They do not determine exact action durations, concrete breakthrough requirements, or detailed mechanical payoff. The opening method and first breakthrough do not select a permanent specialization.

The opening's two investigations and their broad reward roles are now accepted: the channel yields a useful home arrangement, while the hollow yields a technique useful in the field. Exact effects, names, individual scenes, and costs remain design work.

This is the first-arc design checkpoint: the opening direction, core rules, and activity package are accepted. Remaining details include the method's source, exact improvement effects, how routine objectives are selected and presented, breakthrough requirements, and expedition stop/resume details. The next interview slice concerns the world after the first breakthrough and the structure of later path commitment. Acceptance of the activity package does not settle every proposed mechanic or scene in the working draft.
