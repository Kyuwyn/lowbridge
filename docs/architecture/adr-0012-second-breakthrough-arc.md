ADR 0012: Ridge waystation, recovered teaching, and the second breakthrough

Status: Shared local investigation, introductory-experience requirement, recovered teaching source, breakthrough-before-crossing sequence, four broad starter packages, their teaching and fieldwork sequences, complete breakthrough readiness, four first crossings, lasting return access, shared arrival discoveries, optional early regional travel, the first regional arrival package, and the first-journey landslide-and-bypass investigation accepted; exact encounters, thresholds, supplies, costs, and durations remain in design. Date: 16 October 2026; updated 17 October 2026. Basis: explicit answers Q56-Q60, Q63, Q80-Q85, and Q92-Q95; Q64-Q66 revise the class model and starting roster. Q62 redirected the class model for source analysis rather than approving the proposed three-method package.

| Decision | Accepted direction |
|---|---|
| Current design slice | Foundation through the second breakthrough: develop useful introductions to Body, Sword, Earth, and Formation, then show what acquiring the first class changes. |
| Shared local problem | Investigate an old ridge waystation cut off by disturbed qi. Its accessible outskirts introduce the four starting directions; the first acquired class gives each its own way through. A local mystery and practical passage connect village life to the wider world. |
| Experience required before commitment | Use the intended direction's introductory practice before acquiring its class. All four introductions are explained and available to try; sampling the other three is optional. |
| Second breakthrough and crossing | Complete the second breakthrough as a prepared milestone, gain the chosen path's first substantial capability, and immediately use it to cross the disturbed passage into the waystation. |
| Primary introductory teaching source | Recover texts in the accessible outskirts and establish their use through fieldwork. |
| Waystation's first substantial lead | Point toward another connected region, giving a reason to prepare for wider travel while unfinished local opportunities remain available. Following the lead and relocating remain separate choices. |

The village remains the base under the earlier accepted direction. The ridge investigation must support proceeding after trying the chosen tradition alone. It cannot implicitly require the other three introductions through mandatory evidence, materials, or access. Additional introductory experiences can still provide useful knowledge and alternatives.

Trying one path does not satisfy the hands-on requirement for committing to another. For example, a player who has tried Body Tempering and now wishes to choose Formation must use Formation's introductory practice first. The exact activity-completion condition remains to be specified.

The second breakthrough precedes the crossing. Its preparation must therefore be achievable from accessible places without requiring the capability awarded by that breakthrough. The crossing is the first application of the new capability, not the trial that earns it. Exact preparation, application requirements, and checkpoint capture timing remain open.

Recovered texts are the primary source of the introductions. This preserves the accepted early contacts with cultivators without making them the primary teachers or mandatory arbiters of commitment. Exact texts, discovery locations within the outskirts, completeness of the teachings, and character roles remain open.

Q81 settles the introductory results and field discoveries listed below. The disturbance's cause, exact scenes and technique effects, NPC roster, waystation contents, and route rewards in the [working design](../../design/SECOND-BREAKTHROUGH-DRAFT.md) remain in design.

The existing independent and noncombat progression rules apply to every path. Commitment still precedes the second breakthrough, and does not itself create a checkpoint. Exact breakthrough capture timing and technique compatibility remain open.

Q80 accepts the four broad starter packages:

| Starting direction | Preparation direction | Lasting first-class benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Brief circulation and manageable movement exercises. | Reinforced exertion for physical work and exposed exploration. |
| Sword | Small applications through blade contact. | Precise contact force against understood obstructions; distant force remains Spirit Blade's later development. |
| Earth | Compare stones and ground currents. | Deeper local surveying for routes, deposits, and useful sites. |
| Formation | Steady a small observation area and test a fixed layout. | Limited, temporary arrangements for understood local disturbances. |

Each package has a useful introduction outside the waystation, preparation achievable before the second breakthrough, and its own viable crossing afterward. Only the intended introduction is required; sampling the others remains optional. Exact names, teaching texts, scenes, requirements, effects, costs, and durations remain open.

Q81 accepts the recovered-teaching and fieldwork sequences:

| Direction | Teaching and useful introduction | Discovery and further practice |
|---|---|---|
| Body | Porters' circulation notes; recover a survey marker through brief steadiness. | Discover how load transfer interrupts circulation; practise manageable lifting and carrying with controlled recovery. |
| Sword | Fastening-maintenance notes; expose an inscription through blade contact. | Distinguish the binding from harmless traces; demonstrate separate controlled contact applications. |

| Formation | Temporary field-shelter notes; steady an observation area. | Discover where moving a marker diverts the disturbance; test a small fixed layout with a harmless release direction. |

All sequences are achievable before breakthrough 2. An outing can satisfy several steps when its accomplishments fulfill those conditions, and no sequence requires another introduction. Discoveries explain what further practice can achieve.
