ADR 0025: Lifespan and checkpoints cut

Status: Accepted removal of age, lifespan, the fictional calendar, checkpoints and retry; the bank is unchanged; the pacing targets that survive are provisional and unmeasured. Date: 20 October 2026. Basis: the prior-review complaints recorded in the design-intent report §3.7 and §3.8, its open-gap list §4 and its contradiction list §5.1, and rules 12, 14, 18, 25 and 27 of [`research/arcanum-reference/12-design-rules.md`](../../research/arcanum-reference/12-design-rules.md).

**Context.** The mortality system has produced one exploit, one unresolved ADR contradiction and no evidence.

The exploit is §3.7: "the Degenerate Strategy ('Bank-Full Suicide Scouting')". Retaining the bank across retries let a player hoard time, die deliberately to scout ahead, and come back with the hoard intact, while a player who spent the bank honestly lost it. The accepted fix was to refund bank spent since the checkpoint, recorded in design plan §0 F5, Part A rule 10 and Part F, and §3.7 also records, against that fix, "Simulation: not shown … its route evidence does not exercise retry/refund behavior." So the system's only defence against its own exploit has never been run.

The contradiction is §5.1: [ADR 0002](adr-0002-lifespan-and-retries.md) says current bank survives a retry and "spent banked time stays spent", [ADR 0007](adr-0007-away-time-and-acceleration.md) repeats it, and the design plan, the implementation plan and the README all refund instead. A reader of the ADRs alone gets the wrong rule.

The missing evidence is §4 and §3.8. Age conversion, lifespan extensions, the fictional calendar and display, intermediate dates and representative reserve objectives are all listed as open or provisional, and the real-day pacing the fictional calendar was derived from is itself "a property of the route script, not of the opening". The whole of design plan B.6's arithmetic (1 activity hour = 0.25 fictional years, ≈ 76 hours, ≈ 19 fictional years, Cloudwell at age 28-38) rests on numbers nothing has measured, and the first slice has no mortality content at all: no retry has ever been played, and the only thing age does today is count up.

The reference has no mortality and no checkpoint. Rule 14 (*copy*) names its four independent ladders (tier, skills, wizard level, morality), and none of them is a life. Rule 27 (*copy*) is its whole failure rule: adventures are slot activities with floors, "leaving keeps progress; death costs time only; bosses reset". Rule 25 (*copy*) makes the offline bank a capped resource spent deliberately and grown by progression, with no connection to mortality. Rule 12 (*copy*) prices a tier-up in pointers, a bill and locks, with no life term. Rule 18 (*copy*) puts the pressure somewhere else entirely: caps first, rates second, bills sized to most of a full cap even at tier 4.

**Decision.** Cut it.

1. **No age and no lifespan.** `age` and `lifespan` leave the state, the record grammar, the interface and the save. There is no fictional calendar, no fictional-year display and no conversion from activity time to fictional time.
2. **No checkpoint and no retry.** `checkpoint` and `bank.spentSinceCheckpoint` leave the state; `retry()` leaves the engine surface; the bank-refund rule has nothing to apply to and is withdrawn.
3. **One failure rule, for everything.** An unsuccessful attempt costs the time it spent and the preparation it consumed, keeps every discovery, key, level and attunement, and returns the player to the choice (rule 27, *copy*). [ADR 0005](adr-0005-campaign-progression.md)'s trial setback and [ADR 0008](adr-0008-optional-combat.md)'s combat setback already said exactly this; they are now the whole of it, applying to ordinary obstacles, defining trials and combat alike. Nothing in the campaign ends a journey.
4. **Breakthroughs pay caps, unlocks and bank cap.** The "+5 y / +8 y / +10 y / +15 y" column is void, and so is every longevity reward and lifespan extension.
5. **The bank is unchanged.** [ADR 0007](adr-0007-away-time-and-acceleration.md)'s accrual while nothing runs, one-for-one exchange, prompt delivery to the current objective or next meaningful pause, re-enabling per objective, and a generous cap raised by progression all stand (rule 25, *copy*). Only its "Bank across checkpoint retries" row is void. The §3.7 exploit disappears with the thing it exploited: there is nothing to die into and nothing to restore.
6. **Pacing is stated in ordinary activity time and real days only, and marked provisional.** [ADR 0019](adr-0019-early-campaign-pacing.md)'s Q122 (a meaningful next choice within roughly 8-12 hours of ordinary activity) and Q123 (roughly 3-5 real days through Cloudwell) stand as unmeasured targets. Q116, Q117, Q118 and Q119 (the lifespan-budget target, 10-20 fictional years, age 18 with natural lifespan 80, and the common activity-to-fictional-time scale) are void. Q114's reference play pattern and Q120-Q121's bank rules stand.
7. **Save format v6, and saves before it are not loadable.** The removed fields cannot be reconstructed and the checkpoint snapshot a v5 save carries has no meaning under this decision, so no migration is honest. The loader refuses v1-v5 by version with a plain message and leaves the running game untouched. Export is unchanged. v6 also carries attunement ([ADR 0023](adr-0023-techniques-and-attunement.md)) and keys ([ADR 0024](adr-0024-route-keys.md)) as authoritative state, and recomputes every derived total ([ADR 0021](adr-0021-compounding-vocabulary.md)).

**No divergence from a *copy* rule is created by this decision.** Age, lifespan, checkpoints and retry were Lowbridge additions with no counterpart in the reference, and cutting them moves the design toward rules 14, 25 and 27 rather than away from them. The *copy* rule this decision now leans on, rule 18, was already accepted: the opening starts every resource tiny and capped and sizes bills against a full cap.

**What is given up, honestly.** [ADR 0002](adr-0002-lifespan-and-retries.md)'s stated purpose was that "the lifespan limit discourages indefinite accumulation and makes major commitments consequential". Nothing replaces that as a global clock. What remains is local: caps and bills sized against them (rule 18), the locks a breakthrough closes behind it (rule 12, design plan Part A rule 6), strain budgets on expeditions from B2 (Part A rule 12), and the cost of a failed attempt in time and spent preparation. Whether that is enough pressure is a playtest question, added to design plan Part E, not a settled claim. If it is not enough, the replacement should be a bounded, non-terminal cost at the stage that needs it, never a restored global life.

**Consequences.** Design plan: Part A rule 5 loses its lifespan term; rule 10 becomes the setback rule; rule 12 is the strain budget without the comparison; Part B.2 loses the Lifespan column; Part B.6 is bank-only; Part C.1 loses the Age row; Part C.3's Foundation rewards lose "lifespan +5"; Part D's save sentence loses age and the checkpoint snapshot; Part E's pacing check loses "fictional age at Cloudwell within 28-38"; Part F records the supersession. Implementation plan: `retry()` leaves the engine surface, the state description loses age/lifespan/checkpoint/spent-since-checkpoint, and the evidence steps test the setback rule and the v6 refusal instead of the retry refund. README: the retry sentence goes, the age sentence in the acceleration paragraph goes, and the save paragraph says older saves are not loadable. Engine: deleting the checkpoint snapshot removes the largest object in the save and the only place the engine had to clone whole world state.

**Supersessions.** Supersedes [ADR 0002](adr-0002-lifespan-and-retries.md) in full; that ADR is history, and nothing in it is in force. Amends [ADR 0007](adr-0007-away-time-and-acceleration.md): the bank is unchanged, and only its retry row is void. Amends [ADR 0019](adr-0019-early-campaign-pacing.md): no fictional calendar, no age 18 and lifespan 80, no 0.25 fictional years per activity hour, Q116-Q119 void, Q114 and Q120-Q123 standing and provisional. Supersedes design plan Part A rule 10, Part B.6's conversion line and Part F's "ADR 0002 spent banked time stays spent" row, which together recorded the refund rule this decision withdraws.

Still open: whether any later stage wants a bounded non-terminal cost in place of the cut pressure, and what the Part E playtest question above returns.
