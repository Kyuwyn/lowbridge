ADR 0013: Knowledge and practice across the campaign

Status: Accepted campaign-wide progression rule; concrete limits and content remain in design. Date: 16 October 2026. Basis: explicit answer Q61, extending the opening rule accepted in Q19.

Practice develops the cultivator within current understanding. Throughout the campaign, discoveries and new teachings open deeper growth at meaningful limits. Sustained repetition alone cannot pass a limit that requires further understanding.

This preserves a continuing role for investigation and teaching as methods deepen. Practice still has a substantial role: the cultivator must develop what has been understood into dependable capability. The rule does not require a new discovery for every small improvement or establish a numerical knowledge-point currency, resource cap formula, or universal activity count.

The existing objective stops, one-primary-activity rule, lifespan costs, and time-bank behavior continue to apply. Concrete content must identify what the current method can teach and which obtainable understanding supports further growth. Required knowledge must respect the independent and noncombat progression rules for all three paths. The ridge arc must remain viable after trying only the intended path's introduction.

The [second-breakthrough working design](../../design/SECOND-BREAKTHROUGH-DRAFT.md) proposes path-specific observations and preparation using this rule. Q61 accepts the progression structure, not those individual observations, effects, readiness checks, or costs. Their detailed design and review remain open.

See [ADR 0003](adr-0003-opening-anchors.md) for the opening decisions, [ADR 0012](adr-0012-second-breakthrough-arc.md) for the ridge arc, and the [glossary](../../design/GLOSSARY.md) for the working meaning of a practice limit.
