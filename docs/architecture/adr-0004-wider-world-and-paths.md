ADR 0004: Wider world and path commitment

Status: Second design checkpoint. Wider-world direction, path roster, and broad retraining rules accepted; details remain in design. Date: 16 October 2026. Basis: explicit answers Q24-Q29.

Review note after Q62: the user requested closer alignment with Arcanum's class and “Master of” system before proceeding with the proposed three-method package. The roster, single-path commitment, and replacement-through-retraining rules recorded here are retained as earlier decisions and are now under review. The [source analysis](../../research/arcanum-study-2026-10-16/CLASS-MASTERY-STUDY.md) proposes an adaptation for discussion; no replacement class roster or retraining rule has been selected yet.

The [first arc](../../design/OPENING-DRAFT.md) establishes a shared foundation without selecting a path. The next phase gives the player meaningful alternatives before a consequential commitment, while continuing the accepted home-and-expedition structure.

| Decision | Accepted direction |
|---|---|
| Immediately after the first breakthrough | Deepen the surrounding world. New abilities open local leads and first contacts with cultivators, while the village remains the protagonist's base. |
| Scope of later path commitment | The chosen path defines deepest advancement and signature abilities. Compatible supporting techniques remain learnable elsewhere. |
| Experience before commitment | Learn introductory practices with real uses, applying modest techniques to investigations or practical problems to experience each tradition's approach. |
| Changing a committed path | Keep one committed path at a time. Changing direction is possible through a substantial undertaking requiring deliberate retraining and preparation, with clear costs. |
| First complete campaign roster | Use three paths with deep progression through the shared journey to ascension: Body Tempering, Intent, and Formation. Develop their differences and supporting techniques; final names remain open. |
| Progress retained through retraining | Retain discoveries, home development, general advancement, and earned lifespan extensions while working to develop the new path's advanced abilities. |
| Timing of first commitment | Before the second major breakthrough, after opportunities to try introductory practices; confirmed by Q31 and recorded with campaign progression in ADR 0005. |

These decisions preserve the earlier campaign structure: shared world and major milestones, with path-specific opportunities, solutions, and defining trials. The first foundation does not choose a path. Keeping the village as the next phase's base does not prevent later relocation when opportunities justify it.

Design consequences: local discoveries and home improvements should remain useful after the first breakthrough. Encounters with other cultivators need to help the player understand meaningful alternatives. Supporting techniques must not erase the distinction between a path's signature capabilities and broadly available tools. Exact compatibility rules remain open.

Retraining is part of the continuing character's journey. It preserves shared progress while the new specialization is developed. Exact costs, availability, and the treatment of particular former techniques remain undecided. Exact checkpoint restoration still applies: a retry restores whichever path and retraining state belonged to that checkpoint. This does not establish a manual rewind or a special exception that preserves retraining progress across a retry.

The [path working design](../../design/PATHS-DRAFT.md) develops this accepted roster with proposed examples and tradeoffs. [ADR 0005](adr-0005-campaign-progression.md) records commitment timing, the eight-breakthrough target, and the mixture of prepared milestones and defining trials. Sect membership is now optional for every path, offering distinctive teaching and opportunities while each path remains viable independently; [ADR 0010](adr-0010-sects-and-relationships.md) records this and the small recurring cast. Specific sect teachings and membership terms, exact introductory applications, the meaning of technique compatibility, detailed later breakthrough and trial rules, exact realm structure, and exact post-foundation rewards remain open.
