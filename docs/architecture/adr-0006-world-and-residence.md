ADR 0006: Connected regions and the current residence

Status: Fourth design checkpoint. World structure, residence model, combined residence benefits, relocation carryover, home capacity choices, and focused crafting accepted; detailed rules and content remain in design. Date: 16 October 2026. Basis: explicit answers Q37-Q42. Q39's answer "1/2" is recorded as combining the first two options: cultivation possibilities and room for development.

| Decision | Accepted direction |
|---|---|
| World structure | A few substantial connected regions. Travel opens new areas, while discoveries and abilities give earlier places meaningful new uses. |
| Residence model | One current residence. Concentrate home development where the cultivator lives, with relocation possible when new opportunities justify it. |
| Reasons to choose a residence | Combine distinctive cultivation conditions and practical possibilities with room to expand facilities and support more powerful improvements. |
| Improvements when relocating | Carry reusable investments: equipment and knowledge come with the cultivator, while arrangements tied to the new place take work to establish. |
| Home capacity | Choose a setup within capacity. Select improvements suited to current goals; expanding the home lets it support more. |
| Crafting scope | Focused preparation: make selected equipment and arrangements for exploration, home development, and trials. |

The village at the edge of the wilds remains the opening home and the base immediately after the first breakthrough. These decisions extend that foundation across the full campaign. The eight-breakthrough target does not establish a region or relocation for every stage.

Design consequences: returning to an earlier region should offer useful discoveries, applications, or opportunities. Home development focuses on the current residence and supports the existing one-primary-activity structure. A return expedition does not itself require moving home. Each cultivation path must remain viable within the shared world and residence model.

Residences should offer both useful conditions and development potential. The broad distinction between reusable investments and arrangements tied to a place is accepted. Which improvements belong to each category, how much setup is required, and what remains usable at a former residence still need design. The one-current-residence rule continues to apply.

Still open: the exact number and identities of regions, their connections and access conditions, travel costs, specific residence conditions, specific improvement choices and capacity values, reconfiguration rules, crafting recipes and material sources, relocation requirements and costs, and the treatment of a former residence. The accepted capacity constraint does not yet select its units, numerical budget, furniture slots, a layout grid, or reconfiguration costs. Focused crafting leaves specific recipes and acquisition routes open.
