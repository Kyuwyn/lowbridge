# Arcanum: Theory of Magic — wiki reference notes

**Scope/version.** The wiki Main Page labels the stable release **0.12.0.0**. Every numeric value in these notes is transcribed from that stable wiki snapshot unless explicitly marked **not on wiki**; page-specific revision drift is possible. These notes condense rules and progression, not per-item data catalogs already available elsewhere.

## 1. New-player flow / recommended opening — [Guide:New players](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Guide:New_players), [Classes](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Classes)

The guide presents discovery rather than a mandatory build: it says there are no wrong choices, and the player should skip the advice if not stuck or frustrated. It gives no clocked first-hour schedule and no universal first-purchase order (**not on wiki**).

| Order | Community guidance |
|---|---|
| 1 | Run the first available tasks and passive activities to earn resources. Spend them on upgrades/furniture, then train newly available skills; repeat this loop. Hold **Enter** after an instant task to repeat it. |
| 2 | Leave **Auto Focus** enabled unless deliberately training something else. Lock tasks/activities when the interface warns about unusual costs; hide irrelevant tasks with the gear. Ignore newly opened tabs until their systems are useful. |
| 3 | Buy the first available apprentice progression: **Purchase Scroll** (Apprentice costs Gold 20 and Research 10), which opens the Home/Alcove, Mage Lore, chores/errands and early furniture. The page tables disagree on the Small Chest Gold figure (40 on the furniture/unlock row versus 75 on the class unlock row); preserve the conflict rather than inventing a single value. |
| 4 | Train the skills that furniture/tasks expose. The guide example is source-tagged furniture for early skills, such as Potted Milkweed Plant → Herbalism. |
| 5 | Take an apprenticeship/job route after its prerequisites are visible. Early job classes include Smith (Crafting), Falconer (Animal Handling), Herbalist (Herbalism), and Scribe (Scribe Scroll). |
| 6 | For class specialization, choose a primary classline, for example Knowledge/Master of Knowledge, take **3** classes from that line where possible, then use the remaining **3** slots for spells/activities or complementary lines. Three from one line maximizes specialties; a higher-tier class can require a lower-tier class. |
| 7 | Before a difficult adventure, memorize a short autocast list; manually cast or augment for hard encounters. Repeating non-combat activities can award resources or raise caps. |
| 8 | Choose morality deliberately: pursue full Virtue, full Evil, or balance/neutrality. Read the irreversible-choice warnings below before Study, Bind Spellbook, tier graduation, and morality paths. |

**Early progression details.** Waif → Apprentice requires Research 10 and Gold 20. Apprentice opens Mage Lore, Home/Alcove, Do Chores and Run Errands; it also locks Clean Stables. Neophyte requires the Leaky Cellar and Bind Spellbook and raises Research Max to 125; its listed cost is Gold 150, Research 175, Arcana 3. Job routes expose Leaky Cellar, Stony Hills and Treffil Woods Edges; Edges must be visited before Bind Spellbook/Making Spellbook if Red Adept is desired.

## 4. Combat rules beyond the in-game glossary — [Player](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Player), [Monsters](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Monsters), [Barrier](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Barrier), [Stressors](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Stressors), [Adventures](https://theoryofmagic.miraheze.org/wiki/Adventures)

| Rule | Wiki data |
|---|---|
| Defense / dodge / accuracy | Defense reduces incoming enemy damage. Dodge can avoid an attack entirely. Accuracy/to-hit is matched against enemy Dodge; more Accuracy makes Dodge less effective. The wiki does not publish a numeric hit/dodge/defense formula (**not on wiki**). |
| Speed / turns | Speed reduces turn delay and makes attacks happen faster. Exact speed-to-turn-time formula is **not on wiki**. |
| Damage types | Physical, Force, Blunt, Slash, Pierce, Mana, Fire, Water, Nature, Air, Chaos, Earth, Shadow, Light and Spirit are listed. |
| Damage over time | No universal DoT formula is published. Example data: Blazing Heat gives Conflagration DoT min 0.03/max 0.06 and Searing Radiance min 0.02/max 0.04. |
| Barrier | Barrier is depleted before Life. Out-of-combat/rest regeneration is 10% of Barrier Max per second; Mana Veil gives Barrier Max × 0.05 per second. |
| Stress types | Befuddlement, Unease, Weariness, Frustration and Madness. Maximum starts at 10. Concentration adds 1 per level; Demure Gelding adds 1; Wizard’s Hall Prestige adds 0.1% per point. |
| Stress in locales | Encounters raise stress; resting lowers it. Composure lowers all five by 0.1 per level. Calming Murmurs lowers Befuddlement/Frustration/Madness by 0.2; Soothing Breeze lowers Unease/Frustration/Weariness by 0.2; Dreams lowers Weariness by 0.1; Clarity lowers Befuddlement by 0.5. |
| Adventure loss/recovery | Locales auto-progress through encounters; reaching maximum stress ejects the player. A battle completes when all enemies die and is lost when the player dies. After a loss, with full Life, enemy HP is preserved. Entering another unfinished dungeon/locale resets the prior unfinished run. |
| Clashes | Usually 1–3 battles; clash progress is not saved. |
| Companion/summon targeting | The wiki gives per-entity behavior, not a universal priority rule (**not on wiki**). Rabbit Companion taunts and attacks an enemy; summon entries commonly say they damage/taunt enemies. Caduceus equipment explicitly targets minions. |

The pages Combat, Dungeons, Companions and Minions are empty or do not provide a separate stable rules page; the linked player/monster/barrier/stressor/adventure pages are the available sources.

| System | Wiki rule |
|---|---|
