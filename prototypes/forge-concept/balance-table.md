# Forge prototype: balance table

**Spoilers.** This lists the result of every makeup under both methods. If you are the
playtester, play `prototype.html` first.

Seals are the three doors' tiers added up (shut 0, open 1, wide open 2), in the order
Furnace Gate / Deep Well / Listening Hall. Days are the cheapest route under that method.
The budget is 9 days.

## What the numbers were tuned for

- Each method has exactly one best makeup within the budget, and the two differ.
- Neither best makeup is the method's obvious on-theme set.
- Dropping any ingredient from a best makeup lowers its score, so none is filler.
- Some makeups cost more than the budget, including one that would have tied the best.

## Known weakness to watch for in the debrief

Under each method the best makeup looks like the *other* method's theme. That makes the
answer non-obvious, but it may read as the method being backwards. If it does, that is a
finding about pillar 2 (methods matter), not a bug in the table.
