# Lowbridge: first playable notes

Start a new journey at [the local preview](http://127.0.0.1:4173). Play at your own pace until Foundation or until you feel stuck. The opening is provisional; your decisions and impressions matter more than reproducing a scripted route.

**At the end:** use **Export** in the save controls. Keep the JSON with your notes. For a second route, export first, then start a new journey. The supplied [fresh-start save](fresh-start.json) also begins from the village with an empty bank; Foundation exports are optional reference snapshots and contain spoilers.

**Session:** date / roughly how long you played / any time away / channel, hollow, or both / reached Foundation or where you stopped.

Answer these in order; short answers are enough.

1. **Practice, wall, discovery.** Did growing through the runway, receiving the level benefits, hitting the limit and moving it through discovery feel like cultivation? Which moment made that work or fail?
2. **The two routes.** Did the channel's preparation and the hollow's perception feel different? Did you follow a preference, or simply do everything available?
3. **Stopping and returning.** Try leaving a chosen objective running and returning later, starting with little or no bank. Did it stop where you expected? Was the unused time and subsequent acceleration understandable?
4. **Foundation.** Did the bill, change in livelihood and new leads feel like a threshold? Mill work closes when you first complete ferry hauling. Did anything feel taken away without a worthwhile replacement?
5. **Getting stuck.** When an action was unavailable or practice hit a cap, did its explanation give enough context to decide what to try? Quote any confusing text or any “you don't know yet” line.
