# Independent review request

Please review this proposed browser idle RPG, inspired by Arcanum / Theory of Magic and themed around cultivation toward immortality. Treat the current design as something to examine critically, not a conclusion to endorse. The shaping interview has ended; the intended next step is a bounded first playable slice from the village opening through shared foundation.

The package contains the original reference study, a focused class/mastery study, accepted design decisions, historical alternatives, and provisional pacing calculations. Begin with the package README so that superseded ideas are not mistaken for current requirements. Developer capacity and delivery dates are not established here; state any staffing assumptions used in scope judgments.

## Questions

1. Does the design retain the useful qualities of Arcanum's progression and cumulative classes while establishing a distinct game? Identify meaningful build differences, dominant options, or choices that are only nominal.
2. What is likely to engage the player between breakthroughs? Examine whether investigation, preparation, work, and cultivation produce decisions or repetitive checklists. Challenge the proposed activity and decision rhythm where warranted.
3. Are progression, the time bank, knowledge limits, lifespan, and checkpoint retries coherent together? Identify contradictions, prerequisite cycles, awkward incentives, and cases missed by the two paper journeys.
4. What drives implementation and content-authoring scope? Recommend concrete simplifications or areas worth expanding, distinguishing essential systems from expensive breadth. Avoid deriving a development schedule from line counts or document length.
5. What should the first playable slice actually test? Rank uncertainties requiring play evidence and identify any major decisions that genuinely need revisiting before implementation.

## Useful response format

- A short overall assessment, including supported strengths and weaknesses.
- Prioritized findings with references to relevant filenames and sections, the reason each finding matters, and a concrete alternative where possible.
- Separate documented facts, your interpretations, and your recommendations. Treat accepted decisions as current intent that can be challenged, and provisional values as hypotheses.
- A bounded recommendation for the first playable slice, plus what should wait.

Please do not assume the source research or arithmetic model establishes real-player pacing, balance across all class histories, enjoyment, or production readiness. If a claim cannot be assessed from this package, identify the missing evidence rather than treating it as either verified or disproven.
