## Reading the history correctly

The documents preserve the interview, including alternatives and superseded proposals. Earlier passages are not all simultaneous requirements. In particular:

- The early three-path commitment/retraining concept was superseded by cumulative class history and overlapping masteries. The current four starting directions are Body, Sword, Earth, and Formation. `PATHS-DRAFT.md` and historical sections of other drafts retain the earlier exploration.
- Q123 accepts a compact working pace of roughly **3–5 real days through Cloudwell** for the two specified examples, superseding the former focused 7–10-day and curious 10–14-day endpoints. Foundation still targets the first day. Intermediate calendar targets need alignment.
- Exact timers, prices, home bonuses, age conversion, bank capacity, and visit assumptions in the numerical examples remain **provisional**. Acceptance of the overall pacing direction did not approve those constants.
- The first four breakthroughs are developed in detail. Breakthroughs 5–8 remain a campaign framework. No implemented prototype or measured player pacing is claimed.
- The Arcanum studies describe a pinned source snapshot and bounded observations, not an exhaustive playthrough or proof of every possible build's reachability. The source checkout itself is not bundled; the reports link to the pinned public source. Supporting census data, the census script, and the saved upstream license are included.
