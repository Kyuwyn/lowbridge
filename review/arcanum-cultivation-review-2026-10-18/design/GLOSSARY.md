The campaign uses a few such regions; new areas become accessible through travel, while abilities and discoveries give earlier places new uses. Exact count, boundaries, and identities remain open. | Broad world structure agreed; terminology provisional |
| Primary activity | The cultivator's current work, practice, preparation, or exploration. Only one runs at a time; home improvements benefit the activity underway. | Agreed |
| Expedition | An undertaking away from home with a chosen destination and purpose. Known work can proceed between visits; a discovery creating a meaningful new choice pauses the outing. In the first arc, equipment, techniques, and understood obstacles govern access alongside the cost in time and lifespan; there is no additional energy or food meter. An ordinary obstacle pauses with useful information and preserves discoveries; time already spent is the cost. Detailed stop/resume rules remain open. | Role, broad control, and first-arc constraints agreed |
| Practice limit | The extent of growth current understanding can support. Throughout the campaign, discoveries and new teachings open deeper growth at meaningful limits, while practice develops what is already understood. This does not establish a knowledge-point currency or require a new discovery for every small improvement. | Campaign-wide role agreed; concrete limits open |
| Lead | A possibility suggested by observations which the player can choose to investigate and prepare for. The opening offers a small set of useful leads; readiness does not require completing them all. | Opening role agreed |
| Routine | Configured activities that can carry out the player's decisions between visits, respecting one primary activity at a time. A home activity stops at its chosen objective and pauses for the player's next decision. Automation must also stop before exhausting lifespan. Objective selection and presentation remain open. | Broad role and home stopping rule agreed |
| Discovery | Information, knowledge, or an opportunity that changes the player's understanding or available possibilities. It is a design goal, not yet a formal reward category. | Working concept |
| Realm | Provisional term for a substantial stage of cultivation. The campaign targets eight major breakthroughs, including first foundation and final ascension. Realm names, the mapping of realms to this ladder, internal structure, and detailed transition rules remain undecided. | Provisional terminology; breakthrough count agreed |
| Major breakthrough | A substantial advancement associated with the campaign's lifespan extension and retry milestones. The campaign targets eight including first foundation and final ascension, with a mix of prepared milestones and defining trials. The first succeeds on a deliberate attempt once its required preparations are satisfied. The second is a prepared milestone whose new capability is immediately used to cross into the ridge waystation. Later trials depend on preparation and choices, without a separate random failure roll. The first three breakthroughs are prepared milestones; Q74 places the first defining trial at breakthrough 4. Exact requirements, state changes, and later trial placements remain open. | Broad role, count, mixed structure, early trial timing, and outcome direction agreed |
| Defining trial | A selected major transition requiring the player to choose and carry out a suitable approach, within a campaign that also includes prepared milestones. Preparation and choices determine the outcome, without a separate random failure roll. An unsuccessful trial while lifespan remains costs time and repeatable preparation while retaining established advancement and discoveries. It must support viable noncombat progression. Q74 places the first at breakthrough 4, using pre-advancement capabilities after class readiness. Exact interaction, prerequisites, costs, and later placements remain open. | Broad role, outcome, setback, and first placement agreed; content open |
| Combat | An optional approach to encounters. The player prepares equipment, techniques, and an approach; the encounter proceeds automatically without directing every exchange. An ordinary unsuccessful attempt with lifespan remaining costs time and used preparation while retaining established advancement and discoveries. Detailed resolution, encounter content, and retreat controls remain open. | Broad control and ordinary setback agreed; detailed mechanics open |
| Noncombat route | A viable way to achieve major progression through approaches other than fighting. Required resources, knowledge, longevity, and access must not secretly force combat. | Campaign role agreed; concrete routes open |
| Shared foundation | The basic cultivation established by the first major breakthrough. It supports several future paths without committing the protagonist to one of them. | Opening role agreed |
| Lifespan | The finite life available for simulated activities before age exhaustion ends an attempt. | Agreed |
| Age | Life consumed through simulated activity, rather than simply by being away from the game. Activities accelerated with banked time consume lifespan normally. Starting value and units remain open. | Broad rule agreed |
| Banked time | Unused real time saved while no activity runs, whether the game is open or closed. This includes completed objectives, decision pauses, and voluntarily stopped activities; time used to advance activity is not also credited. It can accelerate player-chosen activities, respecting normal lifespan costs and activity stops. A generous fixed cap is intended to cover a few days; its exact value remains to be tested. Acceleration is enabled for one current objective and ends at that objective or a meaningful pause. The next activity requires enabling it again. Checkpoint retries keep the current bank balance: spent time stays spent and later accrual remains. Exact speed, cap values, presentation, and implementation remain open. | Broad accrual, storage, spending, and retry rules agreed; details open |
| Checkpoint | The character and world state restored exactly when retrying from the last major breakthrough. Later character/world gains and choices are undone. Before the first breakthrough, lifespan exhaustion instead restores the opening starting state. The current time-bank balance is retained rather than rewound. | Restore scope agreed; breakthrough capture timing open |
| Attempt | Context-dependent design discussion term: it may mean progress since a checkpoint or one undertaking such as a trial. An unsuccessful trial with life remaining preserves advancement and discoveries; a lifespan-exhaustion retry restores character and world state from the checkpoint exactly, retaining the current time bank. | Working terminology; distinguish trial setback from checkpoint restore |
| Ascension | The final achievement of immortality and passage beyond the mortal world, concluding the campaign with an epilogue. | Agreed |

Distinctions to preserve: witnessing qi is not yet reliable mastery; obtaining the opening method and completing the first major breakthrough do not commit the protagonist to a path; learning from a failed attempt does not carry rewards or journal progress across a checkpoint restore. Exact mechanical boundaries will be settled in later interview slices.
