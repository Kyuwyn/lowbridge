The first complete campaign uses three broad paths whose differences lie in the decisions they encourage: develop the self, direct a precise action, or prepare the surroundings. Each needs a complete route through the shared campaign to ascension, distinctive advanced capabilities, and meaningful choices within its progression. Three is the accepted roster size for this campaign scope.

**Accepted traditions — abilities and examples still proposed**

| Working identity | Characteristic choice | Introductory practice with a real use | Depth reserved for commitment | Tradeoff to preserve |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body Tempering | Develop yourself enough to take a direct approach. | Controlled exertion and steadiness help with difficult footing, bracing an obstruction, or transporting a useful finding. | Transform what the body can withstand and accomplish, eventually sustaining cultivation under conditions that overwhelm an ordinary practitioner. | Capability travels with the cultivator, but endurance does not explain an unfamiliar phenomenon or resolve every delicate obstruction. |
| Intent | Understand what must change, then apply a precise technique. | Direct a small application of qi to release a constriction or separate an obstruction while preserving what is worth studying. | Refine and sustain intent to act on deeper bindings and connections, beyond simply applying greater force. | Powerful when the cause is understood; diffuse or poorly understood problems demand further investigation or another approach. |
| Formation | Decide whether a place is worth preparing and what that preparation will enable. | Arrange a small area so its qi becomes steadier and easier to observe or use. | Redirect complex local flows and establish conditions that make difficult undertakings possible. | Preparation takes activity time and is often tied to a place; useful for difficult or repeated work, less immediately helpful on unfamiliar ground. |

Intent is the selected broad identity. Sword practice is one possible expression, not a selected combat system or a requirement that all intent techniques use a weapon. Its abilities need concrete limits; intent must not become a universal answer to every problem.

**A shared situation as a design test**

As an illustrative later encounter, a mountain passage is disrupted by a dangerous qi current. A body cultivator might develop the capacity to cross and anchor themselves safely. An intent cultivator might investigate and sever the particular binding sustaining the disturbance. A formation cultivator might prepare a small area that redirects the flow long enough to proceed.

These are proposed approaches to the same authored situation. They need understandable prerequisites and comparable value; one must not simply bypass the work expected from the others. There should also be ordinary preparation and compatible supporting techniques where appropriate. The example does not require an elemental damage system, a spatial puzzle, or an action combat interface.

**Learning before choosing**

The accepted next phase retains the village as the base while new local leads introduce cultivators. First commitment occurs before the second major breakthrough and requires practical use of the intended path's introduction. All three introductions are explained and available; sampling the other two is optional. The ridge waystation's accessible outskirts introduce the traditions through a shared investigation. Trying one should provide a useful result even if the player later commits elsewhere. Recovered texts in the accessible outskirts are the primary teaching source, and fieldwork establishes their use. Exact texts and acquisition scenes remain open.

The first arc's home arrangement and perception technique remain shared foundational discoveries. They do not secretly choose Formation or Intent. Basic useful arrangements should remain available outside Formation; advanced specialist depth concerns what can be accomplished with them. Basic perception must support discovery for every path.

Each path should eventually offer both a distinctive cultivation practice and a defining application or trial. Shared encounters may support different approaches without requiring a separate location chain for each tradition. Exact branches, abilities, trials, and counts remain open.

All three paths follow the campaign-wide knowledge rule accepted in Q61: practise within current understanding, with discoveries and new teachings opening deeper growth at meaningful limits. [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-knowledge-and-practice.md) records the rule; specific observations and practice limits remain content design.

**Accepted retraining direction**

One committed path is active at a time, with a substantial undertaking of retraining and preparation required to change it. Clear costs are essential. Retraining preserves discoveries, home development, general advancement, and earned lifespan extensions while the character works to develop the new path's advanced abilities. What happens to particular old techniques, how compatibility works, and the time and material costs remain open. Retraining time remains subject to the ordinary activity-based aging rule.

The ordinary checkpoint rule restores saved character and world state exactly; the current time-bank balance is retained under ADR 0007. Retraining does not automatically move a checkpoint, provide manual rewind, or preserve progress that happened after a restored checkpoint. The timing of checkpoints around major advancement remains a separate unresolved detail.

**Sect affiliation**

Sect membership is optional for every path. Joining offers distinctive teaching and opportunities, while Body Tempering, Intent, and Formation can each reach ascension independently. Path commitment and sect affiliation are distinct choices. Specific sect teachings and the terms of joining or leaving remain open; [ADR 0010](decisions/0010-sects-and-relationships.md) records the accepted direction.

**Scope questions**

This is the second design checkpoint: the roster and broad retraining rules are accepted. The campaign now targets eight major breakthroughs with a mix of prepared milestones and defining trials, and a viable noncombat route through major progression. Each selected path needs to support that direction. Exact realm names and internal structure, the shape of path-specific advancement, detailed combat resolution and frequency, specific crafting recipes, sect benefits and membership terms, compatibility rules, and detailed costs remain undecided. Optional combat uses preparation of equipment, techniques, and an approach followed by automatic resolution. Ordinary unsuccessful attempts with lifespan remaining cost time and used preparation while preserving advancement and discoveries. The accepted crafting scope is focused preparation of selected equipment and arrangements for exploration, home development, and trials, using currency and selected materials. Paid work remains useful alongside expedition rewards; specific jobs and material sources remain to be designed. No independent production or resource-management subsystem is implied by a tradition's theme.
