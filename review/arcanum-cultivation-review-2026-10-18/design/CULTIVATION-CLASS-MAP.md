# Early cultivation class map — first sketch

Updated 18 October 2026. After Q62's [source study](../research/arcanum-study-2026-10-16/CLASS-MASTERY-STUDY.md), the user endorsed the closer direction and requested a map for the first few advancements, with overlapping masteries and hybrid unlocks. **Q64–Q70 settle the early class schedule, starting directions, mastery counting, and breakthrough-3 candidates and readiness package. Q71–Q73 settle the seven-class fourth-breakthrough roster, its mastery gates and contributions, and Golden Cauldron's later entry into alchemy.** Q74–Q78 settle the first defining trial and its practical readiness; Q79 accepts all seven fourth-breakthrough preparation-and-benefit directions. Final names, exact teaching and recipes, detailed applications, numerical values, costs, and durations remain open. [ADR 0014](decisions/0014-early-class-advancements.md), [ADR 0015](decisions/0015-cumulative-class-masteries.md), [ADR 0016](decisions/0016-third-breakthrough-classes.md), and [ADR 0017](decisions/0017-fourth-breakthrough-choice-breadth.md) record the accepted scope. This sketches 16 identities across three choices; it is not a complete campaign catalog or authorization to implement.

## Accepted early schedule and mastery mechanism

Shared foundation remains the first major breakthrough and selects no class. Q64 accepts one lasting class choice together with each of the next three major breakthroughs: choice A at breakthrough 2, B at breakthrough 3, and C at breakthrough 4. Earlier class benefits remain part of the build. The class-choice schedule for the rest of the eight-breakthrough campaign is outside this decision. Realm names remain open.

Q66 accepts one contribution to each field listed on an acquired class, retained with its class history. Choosing a class uses that advancement's class choice; a later choice can deepen a specialty or move in a new direction. Whether earlier choices can ever be revised remains open. Replacement-through-retraining is not the accepted structure for ordinary forward advancement through these three choices. Class contributions remain character state subject to the existing checkpoint restore rules.

Mastery totals are qualifications earned through classes, not currency or independently trainable skill levels. Body 2 means two acquired classes contributed Body. Training circulation does not itself award another Body contribution. Teachings, discoveries, useful practice, material preparation, and shared advancement readiness still matter; the displayed mastery conditions are only part of each class's requirements.

Every choice checks the history already earned, before adding its own contributions. A class cannot supply its own prerequisite. Accepted lifespan and trial rules still apply.

## Mastery fields used in this map

Q66 accepts Body, Sword, Earth, Formation, and Spirit in the starting assignments, with Spirit's meaning defined below. Q67 adds the accepted Alchemy and Body contributions from Pill Refiner. Detailed applications and benefits remain in design.

| Field | What it concerns in this sketch |
|---|---|
| Body | Internal circulation, physical resilience, and sustained exertion. |
| Sword | Cultivated cutting and precise delivery of force, with exploration, craft, and combat applications. |
| Earth | Mineral structures, ground currents, and stability within terrain. |
| Formation | External patterns and arrangements that shape qi in a place or prepared object. |
| Spirit | Projecting awareness and will beyond the body, distinct from the basic qi perception shared by cultivators. |
| Alchemy | Controlled transformation of ingredients into cultivation preparations. |

Spirit is the accepted shared field for the starting Sword and Formation classes. Its proposed applications include projected sword intent and will extended through a formation; ordinary concentration alone does not qualify. It does not establish souls, summoned creatures, or a companion system. Intent remains a possible technique family rather than a seventh mastery here. The initial assignments do not fix the whole campaign's fields or exclude other elements later.

Spirit Alchemist combines different earlier identities: Sword Disciple or Formation Student supplies Spirit; Pill Refiner supplies Alchemy. Mountain Heart can deepen the combination introduced by Mountain Vessel, or unite Earth Listener's Earth with Pill Refiner's Body. Its teaching must therefore be accessible without previously being Mountain Vessel.

These mastery requirements inspect the accumulated history before the class at breakthrough 4 is acquired, not a required sequence of named predecessors. Choosing Golden Cauldron cannot supply Alchemy for Spirit Alchemist at the same breakthrough. Spirit Warden's introductory ward teaching must be accessible to Sword → Spirit Blade with Spirit 2 and no Formation mastery. Stone Ward must likewise remain accessible to Earth → Earth Array without an unlisted Spirit requirement. These are constraints on practical preparation still to be designed, not extra mastery gates.

### Accepted Golden Cauldron entry

The original sketch required Alchemy ≥1. Q72 replaces that gate with entry from any current history through accessible refining teaching and practical preparation. A newcomer learns extraction, controlled heating, and a stable beginner preparation before attempting the deeper study. These introductory activities teach skills; they do not award Alchemy mastery. Required sources and equipment must be accessible without a sect, combat, a particular residence, or prior Alchemy.

An existing Pill Refiner keeps all earlier class benefits and receives credit for qualifying preparation already completed. Both routes still need the Golden Cauldron-specific deeper teaching and preparation; the class's future capabilities cannot be its own prerequisites. Exact exercises, recipes, prices, and durations remain open.

Acquisition adds only Alchemy +1. A prior Pill Refiner reaches Alchemy 2 and retains its earlier Body contribution and conditioning benefits. A newcomer reaches Alchemy 1 and gains no retroactive Pill Refiner benefits or Body mastery. Q73 accepts the other mastery gates in the table. This supplies an alchemical alternative to all five formerly single-option histories while preserving their specialist route; its practical value and lifespan cost still need design and testing.

## Mastery beyond another class

Candidate uses include Body 2 opening an advanced circulation teaching; Earth 2 revealing deeper mineral survey opportunities; Formation 2 qualifying a stronger home arrangement; and Spirit plus Alchemy qualifying a preparation that preserves an otherwise lost observation. Learning and practising the relevant technique, or preparing an arrangement, remains separate from having the tags.

These are examples, not additional approved subsystems. Reuse existing activities, investigations, focused crafting, and residence development. Every major progression route needs accessible teaching and materials without compulsory combat or sect membership.
