Campaign progression — longer ladder working design

Status: Eight-breakthrough working target, a mix of prepared milestones and defining trials, and trial outcome and setback rules accepted, 16 October 2026. Q74 places the first defining trial at breakthrough 4 after three prepared milestones; [ADR 0018](decisions/0018-first-defining-trial.md) records its timing. [ADR 0005](decisions/0005-campaign-progression.md) records campaign progression and viable noncombat routes. The earlier five-breakthrough proposal is superseded. The individual stage functions below, trial content and placements beyond breakthrough 4, interactions, and preparation costs remain proposed or open. Earlier three-path examples below are historical design inputs; the [cumulative class map](CULTIVATION-CLASS-MAP.md) governs current early specialization.

The campaign follows one cultivator from an ordinary mortal to ascension beyond the mortal world, ending with an epilogue. The target is several weeks at ordinary play speed, supported by short purposeful visits. The opening foundation is targeted for a few short visits roughly within the first day. These remain untested pacing targets.

**Accepted count: eight major breakthroughs; stage functions proposed**

The count includes first foundation and final ascension, leaving six intermediate breakthroughs. The labels describe proposed functions, not canonical realm names. They should change available activities and opportunities without requiring a new subsystem for each stage.

| Major breakthrough | Proposed practical change | What makes the stretch useful |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Establish foundation | Sustain reliable cultivation and perception. | The accepted mortal opening: village work, two investigations, and reconstruction of a working practice. |
| 2. Establish the committed method | Gain the first substantial signature application of Body, Intent, or Formation. | Deeper local leads, first cultivator contacts, practical introductions, and informed commitment. The village remains the base in this stretch. |
| 3. Carry the method into adversity | Apply the method reliably beyond favorable practice conditions. | Prepare for demanding circumstances and use the specialization on journeys where its elementary form would not suffice. |
| 4. Understand deeper connections | Recognize relationships between phenomena that previously seemed separate or misleading. | Revisit known places with new understanding, revealing connected investigations and more informed approaches. |
| 5. Change difficult conditions | Address the source of a persistent qi disturbance or other substantial obstacle. | Work toward a lasting change in a bounded location, giving that place new uses or opportunities. The precise scale must distinguish this from earlier practical preparations. |
| 6. Reconcile interacting demands | Use advanced specialization and compatible techniques where solving one difficulty affects another. | Choose an approach that achieves the intended result while preserving something else that matters. |
| 7. Prove the completed mortal practice | Establish a workable approach to the final undertaking through mature use of the chosen path. | Prepare for ascension with a defining application or trial. The character is exceptionally capable but remains mortal, with finite life. |
| 8. Ascend | Achieve immortality and leave the mortal world. | Complete the final undertaking while sufficient life remains; the epilogue concludes the campaign. |

With this working count, breakthroughs 1–7 supply recovery milestones under the existing retry rule, while breakthrough 8 ends the campaign. Exact checkpoint capture timing remains open. Choosing a path or retraining does not automatically add a recovery point.

The accepted structure mixes prepared milestones with defining trials at selected transitions. Many breakthroughs follow from completed preparation; selected major transitions require choosing and carrying out a suitable approach. The first breakthrough retains its assured-success rule once prepared. Q59 fixes the second breakthrough as a prepared milestone; its new capability is immediately applied to crossing into the ridge waystation, as recorded in [ADR 0012](decisions/0012-second-breakthrough-arc.md). Later trial outcomes depend on preparation and choices, with no separate random failure roll. An unsuccessful trial while lifespan remains costs time and repeatable preparation, preserving established advancement and discoveries. Later trial placements, interactions, and concrete preparation costs remain open. Real-time controls have not been selected.

**Three expressions of the same ladder**

Body Tempering can develop from controlled exertion to enduring difficult conditions, deeper physical transformation, and accomplishing demanding feats through a cultivated body. Intent can develop from precise applications to sustaining purpose under interference and acting on deeper connections while preserving what must remain. Formation can develop from stabilizing a small area to redirecting connected flows and reconciling conflicting conditions through preparation.

These are proposed capability directions within a shared world. Each selected path needs a complete and viable noncombat route through major progression. Optional combat may offer another opportunity or distinctive reward, but indispensable knowledge, access, longevity, and resources cannot all be locked behind fighting. Under [ADR 0008](decisions/0008-optional-combat.md), the player prepares equipment, techniques, and an approach before the encounter proceeds automatically. An ordinary unsuccessful attempt with lifespan remaining costs time and used preparation while preserving advancement and discoveries.

**Play and scope between breakthroughs**

Throughout the campaign, practice develops the cultivator within current understanding. Discoveries and new teachings open deeper growth at meaningful limits; [ADR 0013](decisions/0013-knowledge-and-practice.md) records Q61. Concrete limits still need design and do not require a separate discovery for every small improvement.

Each stretch should support choices about investigations, practice, supporting techniques, home investment, expeditions, and path-specific opportunities. The proposal is to recognize sufficient advancement without requiring every optional lead to be completed. That principle is accepted for the opening; its precise application later remains design work.

The longer ladder should make the climb itself more substantial. It should not reduce each stage to the same preparation checklist with larger numbers. Intermediate stages can be narrower than the opening and finale. The accepted world structure uses a few substantial connected regions, with discoveries and abilities giving earlier places meaningful new uses. The cultivator develops one current residence and can relocate when new opportunities justify it. [ADR 0006](decisions/0006-world-and-residence.md) records these decisions. Residences combine distinctive cultivation possibilities with room for stronger facilities. Reusable equipment and knowledge carry across moves; arrangements at the new site require work. Home improvements compete for capacity, with expansion allowing more. Crafting focuses on selected equipment and arrangements for exploration, home development, and trials, using currency and selected materials. Paid work remains useful throughout the campaign, providing dependable earnings toward chosen purchases alongside expedition rewards; [ADR 0009](decisions/0009-work-and-crafting-resources.md) records these decisions. Exact geography, residence conditions, capacity values, reconfiguration rules, recipes, and moving costs remain open.

Eight is the accepted working target for campaign scope. A completed content inventory and development estimate still require further design. Exact counts of locations, techniques, jobs, equipment, upgrades, encounters, and trials remain open. The several-week pacing target needs evaluation against the choices and interruptions each stage actually contains.

**Sects and recurring characters**

Joining a sect is optional and offers distinctive teaching and opportunities. All three paths retain a complete independent route to ascension. A small recurring cast develops through shared experiences and consequential choices, opening further stories or leads. [ADR 0010](decisions/0010-sects-and-relationships.md) records these decisions. Specific institutions, characters, membership terms, and arcs remain in design.

**Commitment and retraining**

First commitment occurs before the second major breakthrough, after opportunities to try introductory practices. The first substantial advancement beyond foundation expresses the chosen path. The number of introductions required and the exact commitment event remain open.

Retraining preserves discoveries, home development, general advancement, and earned lifespan extensions while the new specialization is developed. Its work consumes activity time under the normal aging rule. Detailed costs, availability, and technique compatibility remain open.

**Accepted trial outcomes and setbacks**

Preparation and choices determine a defining trial's outcome. A suitable approach carried out successfully earns the breakthrough, with no separate random failure roll. Trial decisions must fit short visits and the activity-based aging rule. Their exact interaction and the information presented before an attempt remain open.

If a trial is unsuccessful while lifespan remains, the cost is time and repeatable preparation. Established advancement and discoveries remain, allowing the player to use what the attempt revealed to prepare another approach. This does not automatically require consumable supplies, injuries, or a recovery subsystem. If the undertaking exhausts lifespan, the existing exact checkpoint retry rule applies. Trial setbacks do not create an exception that carries discoveries across that restore.

The noncombat progression rule applies to every defining trial and to ascension. Concrete prerequisites, approaches, repeatable preparation, costs, and trial placements still require design.

The major stages need distinct functions and visible consequences. Names and exact effects remain to be refined within the accepted count and broad breakthrough structure.

**Consolidated scope and remaining design**

The eight stage functions above remain proposals. They need concrete transformations and discoveries that distinguish one stage from the next. Further work includes exact geography, realm names, trial placements and interactions, economic details, time accounting, ending presentation, and implementation approach. Exact action durations and numerical lifespan budgets require later pacing work; the campaign-duration target remains untested.
