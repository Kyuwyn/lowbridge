ADR 0019 — Early campaign reference pace

Status: Accepted pacing targets, lifespan reserve, fictional span, mortal starting values, early time-scale rule, bank exchange/delivery, and regular decision opportunities; not validated balance. Updated 18 October 2026. Basis: Q113 selects this design slice; Q114 accepts the focused journey's reference pattern and dates; Q115–Q116 accept the curious journey's calendar target and lifespan reserve; Q117 accepts the focused journey's fictional span; Q118 accepts the starting values; Q119 accepts a common activity-to-fictional-time scale through breakthrough 4; Q120–Q121 accept one-for-one bank exchange and prompt delivery to the current objective or meaningful pause; Q122 accepts the normal decision rhythm; Q123 supersedes the original Cloudwell dates with a compact 3–5-day reference.

The early campaign now has enough defined teaching, preparation, travel, and trial work to establish intended pacing before assigning individual durations. The [pacing draft](../PACING-DRAFT.md) compares a focused journey with a more exploratory one using the same class history, so optional work and setbacks can be considered separately from differences between builds.

Q114 accepts the following reference scenario: a fresh start without preloaded banked time, roughly two or three short visits a day, and spending earned banked time when useful. The player follows visible leads without needing a guide or perfect choices. This is a balancing reference, not a required attendance schedule.

| Advancement | Earlier Q114 calendar target — historical after Q123 |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Within the first day. |
| Breakthrough 2 and first class | Days 2–3. |
| Breakthrough 3 and second class | Days 4–5. |
| Cloudwell, breakthrough 4, and third class | Days 7–10. |

The table records the earlier focused targets. Q123 below governs the current compact schedule, with intermediate dates to be aligned. These remain intentions to test, not measured completion estimates, deadlines, or fixed activity timers. The complete eight-breakthrough campaign still targets several weeks. A different visit pattern, saved bank, elective projects, or delayed choices can change elapsed calendar time.

Q115 originally targeted days 10–14 for the exploratory example; Q123 supersedes that date with roughly 3–5 days under the same reference play pattern. The example includes the other opening investigation, an extra introduction, home study, additional basin travel, and one ordinary Cloudwell withdrawal and retry. Q116 accepts enough lifespan to finish each stretch with room for one further modest optional objective, using normal breakthrough extensions without requiring extra longevity rewards or the faster settling draught. All earning, preparation, travel, and used reserves count; age carries forward. Representative reserve objectives, exact age values, and other class histories still need checking.

Q117 accepts roughly 10–20 fictional years for the focused journey from the opening through Cloudwell, separate from its real-day target, revised by Q123 to roughly 3–5 days. This is elapsed age added to the starting age, not a maximum lifespan. The curious journey's age follows its actual activity and benefits rather than its calendar days alone. Q118 sets the authored start at age 18 with an unextended natural lifespan of 80, implying gentle early age pressure and a focused Cloudwell age around 28–38. How recurring people and places acknowledge the elapsed years remains to be designed.

Q118 accepts fixed authored starting values of age 18 and natural lifespan 80: 62 years initially, without adding a character-creation system. Q119 accepts one common scale from normal activity time to fictional elapsed time through breakthrough 4. Equal normal durations have equal age costs across activities; efficiency that shortens required activity also saves lifespan. Banked acceleration shortens the player's wait while preserving the normal age cost, and pauses do not age the cultivator. The exact conversion, breakthrough extensions, activity durations, bank settings, and treatment of aging people and places remain open.

Q120 accepts one-for-one bank exchange: one unused real hour stores one bank hour, and spending it supplies one hour of normal activity progress. Time already used for activity is not credited again. Q121 accepts prompt delivery to the current objective, stopping at completion or its next meaningful decision and retaining unused balance. If the bank runs out first, unfinished work continues at ordinary speed. Spending must be enabled again for another objective or a continuation after a decision pause. Normal lifespan costs and all activity prerequisites remain; the exact cap, controls, and processing implementation remain open.

Q122 accepts regular decision opportunities: the main journey should normally reach a meaningful next choice within roughly 8–12 hours of ordinary activity, with longer commitments as explicitly designed exceptions. A short visit may contain several brief discoveries and preparation choices. This is a pacing target, not a forced interruption or a requirement for extra examinations or confirmation clicks. It must work with an empty bank as well as prompt spending. The provisional workload envelope and fictional-time conversion may be revised around this experience; exact activity durations and calendar fit remain unvalidated.

Q123 accepts the compact working pace: roughly 3–5 real days through Cloudwell for the focused and specified curious journeys, preserving the current content and Q122's regular decisions. This supersedes the former 7–10-day focused and 10–14-day curious Cloudwell targets. Foundation still targets the first day; intermediate dates need alignment with the compact schedule. The full eight-breakthrough campaign's several-week target remains separate. Individual timers, prices, home effects, bank capacity, fictional-time conversion, and modeled visit settings are still provisional.

Real elapsed time, simulated activity, and age remain distinct. Banked acceleration advances chosen work with its normal lifespan cost; unused time and decision pauses do not themselves age the cultivator. The stopping and bank rules in [ADR 0007](0007-away-time-and-acceleration.md) and lifespan/retry rules in [ADR 0002](0002-lifespan-and-retries.md) remain in force. Targets do not authorize bypassing meaningful pauses or choosing new activities automatically.

Still open: aligned intermediate calendar targets for both journeys, representative reserve objectives, age display units and calendar convention, exact activity-to-age conversion, lifespan extensions, activity durations, resource costs, exact bank capacity and presentation, and measured differences between supported class histories. These targets do not establish implementation or release readiness.
